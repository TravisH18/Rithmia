# Creation of Mortals (~8,400PD)
After The Cataclysm and First Flood it was decided that if the gods were to survive they would need to create mortal souls who could assist them in future battles. A debate over giving their creations freewill lasted a full month. Some of these gods had seen first-hand what happens when nothing binds the soul. Others believed nothing should ever bind the soul and the lack of creativity would create a dull world. Backroom deals, between good and neutral deities, pushed the tide towards granting free will to make it possible for these creations to be as strong as they were. 

Here thoughts, and consciousness were breathed into mortal creation for the first time. 

Mortals were placed as one on the planet of Rithmia, each ancestry seen by the gods as altered parameters in a grand supervised learning problem. They were place on Yadrela, An island that is essentially point nemo the furthest landmass from any main continent.
