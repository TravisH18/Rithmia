# Azula Empire (Age of Division)
During the age of division a majority of animal folk and their allies departed from Yadrella and travelled south east and founded The Azula Empire 

They travelled inland via the "Azula Gorge", the river/ocean extension that travels inland from the west and almost bisects the continent. They settled mostly on the north side given the south side, now called "The Bayou" was a swampy expanse of trecherous land. 

They moved into the mountains and spread from their. 

Since the Azula Empire was founded by animal folk most formed packs and travelled together however, their all put forward a tribal representative in the empire and the central government, based in Ruby's Crest, was founded as the first Republic in Rithmia's history. They realized how important the fertile lands surrounding the gorge would be and allowed tribes to spread out with promise that they would supply goods to all who spread through fair trade and commerce.  

The governing council of the republic is known as the Summit Accord and it has 11 members from the 11 land based animal folk subspecies.
