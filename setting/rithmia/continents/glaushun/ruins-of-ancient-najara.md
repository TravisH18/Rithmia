# Ancient Najara
In age of division the great underground kingdom was intellectually advanced empire of snakefolk, the Najara. 

Studied cosmology and astronomy to an extreme degree focusing largely on the plane of earth due to contacting both xiomorns and dao while carving out their empire underground. This focus heavily shifted to far realm, the Rithmiasphere and extraterrestrial planets and systems after they discovered stardust deep underground. 

What happened to the xiomorns. Xiomorns were an ancient esoteric race of underdark dwellers. They forged mighty artifacts and crafted the crystal crossroads to connect their empires while skipping over the vast oceans. The snakefolk were a close ally and benefited heavily from the demiplanes made by the xiomorns but when the underground empire was corrupted by an ideology to “reach the stars” they went to war with the kingdom of land and water but first slaughtered the xiomorns and stole as much of their items and research. The expertise of the xiomorns in conjuring demiplanes has led some to believe that a few still exist somewhere. 

In the ruins of ssehethi that the four degens explored they found some of these cosmic biological experiments in which the father of spiders was their attempt at creating a broodspawner which they saw as a way to reproduce faster and eliminate adolescence because the broodspawner would pass on “adaptions” which the Najara believed would mean intelligence and their collective knowledge. However the first time it was used the test went haywire. In an attempt to reverse engineer the broodspawner reptilian dna was mixed with scourge broodling alien/insect DNA creating the first spiders. The “aether chain” that was powering the new life form pumped energy constantly and started an infinite while loop of spider spawning. The one bright side is that this chain kept the father of spiders contained inside its tomb. 

After all this still seeking the star most Najara snakefolk have vanished through abandoned portals to worlds beyond Rithmia. They conquerer the third planet from the sun, a hot tropical planet filled with all kinds of reptilian creatures.
