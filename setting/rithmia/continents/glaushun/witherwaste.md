## History
The Witherwaste was once a vast swampy rainforest ruled by a magocratic alliance who were very curios about the Najara Kingdom whose relics and underground inverted ziggurats dot the landscape. Then an extremely dangerous magic ritual was conducted to not only turn the soon to be Ghoul Emperor into an undead lich but also gave him an advanced form of mummy rot that could turn any mortal into an undead mummy creature. The ghoul emperor made a deal with the black dragons of the swamp to allow them to essentially be generals in his new undead army in turn they act as his strongest forces and he promised to pass along the knowledge of becoming a dracolich. 

In his conquest of the surrounding area the ghoul emperor built massive pyramids atop the relics of the ancient Najaran Kingdom creating a kind of regular octahedron. As his plague armies spread they devour all life in a given area including the flora and fauna. Spreading the desert, showing physical signs of his empires conquest.

### **The Grand Tomb Puzzle: The Eternal Clockwork** 

**Room Description:** 

- **Shape and Structure:** The room is perfectly circular with smooth, obsidian walls that are eerily reflective. The floor is made of polished black stone inlaid with intricate, glowing runes that form a complex pattern resembling a celestial clock. In the center of the room is a large, ancient clockwork mechanism partially embedded in the floor. The mechanism consists of interlocking gears, dials, and a central, ornate clock face. 

- **Atmosphere:** The room is dimly lit by eerie, floating orbs of greenish light that hover just above the floor. The air is heavy with the scent of old metal and an occasional gust of cold wind. 

**Puzzle Mechanics:** 

**1. The Clockwork Mechanism:** 

- **Gears and Dials:** The central mechanism has several large, interlocking gears and dials that need to be adjusted. Each dial has strange symbols and runes, and the gears move slowly but steadily. The clock face has twelve sections, each marked with different arcane symbols. 

**2. The Runic Pattern:** 

- **Floor Runes:** The runes on the floor around the clockwork mechanism are part of a larger, glowing pattern. They seem to correspond with the symbols on the dials. The runes pulse in rhythm, suggesting a hidden sequence or timing mechanism. 

**3. The Puzzle Objective:** 

- **Goal:** To solve the puzzle and open the doors, the characters need to align the clockwork mechanism so that the symbols on the clock face match a specific pattern visible on the floor runes. The correct alignment will activate ancient wards that unlock the doors. 

**4. Clues and Interaction:** 

- **Clue 1 – Rune Alignment:** An inscription on the wall hints at the need to “Set the time to the eternal hour of the pharaoh.” Observing the runes on the floor reveals that they form a clock pattern with a specific arrangement of symbols. Investigation DC 18 

- **Clue 2 – Light and Shadow:** The glowing orbs occasionally shift, casting shadows that briefly align with the symbols on the dials. Observing these shadows helps the characters discern the correct symbols to align. Perception DC 20 

**5. Solution Steps:** 

- **Step 1:** Characters must decipher the pattern of symbols on the floor to determine the correct alignment of the clock face. They need to rotate the dials to match these symbols. Int (History) DC 23 

- **Step 2:** Once the dials are correctly set, the characters need to manipulate the large gears. Each gear must be turned in a specific direction to engage with the clockwork mechanism properly. DC 15 Str (Athletics) check for each gear (6 gears must be turned 

- **Step 3:** As the characters adjust the gears and dials, the clockwork mechanism will slowly align. When the correct symbol pattern is achieved, the clock face will emit a bright, greenish glow. 

**6. Consequences of Failure:** 

- **Trap Activation:** If the characters make too many incorrect adjustments, the room may trigger defensive mechanisms, such as releasing a cloud of necrotic gas or summoning shadowy guardians to challenge the intruders. 

**7. Exit Mechanism:** 

- **Unlocking the Doors:** Once the clockwork mechanism is correctly aligned, the room’s walls will shudder, and the exit doors will slide open with a deep, resonant click. The light orbs will intensify briefly before dimming, signaling the puzzle’s completion. 

This puzzle combines observation, pattern recognition, and a bit of mechanical manipulation, making it an engaging challenge for your players. 

#### Riddle of the Eternal Clockwork: 
```In the heart of darkness, where shadows play, 

A clockwork marvel stands, its secrets in array. 

Twelve faces of power, each marked by fate, 

Align them true, and the doors shall gate. 

Observe the runes, they pulse and glow, 

Their pattern tells the tale, as time does flow. 

Eternal hour of the pharaoh’s reign, 

Set the dials, let the gears strain. 

Light and shadow dance, a fleeting clue, 

Watch them closely, what do they pursue? 

Turn the gears with strength and mind, 

For only then will the path unwind. 

When the clock face glows, the puzzle’s done, 

And the ancient wards, their silence won. 

Open the doors, and step beyond, 

Where secrets lie, and treasures bond. 
```
 
#### Explanation: 

First Stanza: Hints at the room’s atmosphere and the central clockwork mechanism. 

Second Stanza: Directs the players to observe the runes on the floor and their pulsing pattern, which corresponds to the clock face. 

Third Stanza: References the inscription about setting the clock to the "eternal hour of the pharaoh," guiding them to the correct alignment of symbols. 

Fourth Stanza: Encourages the players to watch the light and shadow from the orbs, which provide additional clues. 

Fifth Stanza: Emphasizes the need to turn the gears with both strength and intelligence. 

Sixth Stanza: Describes the visual cue of the clock face glowing when the puzzle is solved and the doors opening.
