# The Vanguard
Possibly started by Troph and Ali after Jades death on the invasion of New Moon Beach. 

The vanguard is the strongest of the common people who fight to overthrow tyranny and eradicate slavery, poverty, starvation. Originally Ali planned to start with Tantras and expand but noticed that as long as people nearby share the cruel tyrannical there will be militias and mercenaries bought to defend it down uprisings. 

This faction acts as a rallying force but does it under the guise of political activism that only launch mass violent revolutions once they can hinder multiple cities in a region at once. The group will do things like feed the hungry open orphanages start small ranches and farms in rural areas since they have money most nobility is happy to accept them. However oppression early ends without violence and the vanguard realize this.
