# Origins
The first dragon seeds were “planted” by Gaia herself and she still refers to dragons as her children.  

The first dragons born from these seeds are now referred to as primeval or first generation dragons. These dragons could not change their forms into humanoids and lived much much longer than later counterparts. Some say the earth dragon god, the last brown dragon is of this generation. 

Second generation dragons were the first able to shapeshift into humanoids  

Third generation is when dragons gained control over a singular element all dragons now are of the third generation  

A draconic generation spans nearly 10000 years
