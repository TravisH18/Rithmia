# Overview
The solar system known as the Rithmiasphere is made up of 4 rocky planets 4 large gassy planets and two dwarf planets. The yellow star in the center is roughly 1.5 time the size of the sun we know earth orbits. Originally when carving out a space for themselves the gods found a space with  10 planets as there were originally 10 of them. Zeisis and Gharian took it upon themselves to hold domain over the two dwarf planets as they were more interested in greater aspects of the Rithmiasphere as a whole. Gharian focused on harnessing the sun. Zeisis founded the river of souls. A place only they knew about since they were gifted the position of The Reaper by a predecessor who simply told them “Everyone takes a float down the stream eventually.”  

 

The planets were held solely by a singular founding deity in the following order. 

The Sun: Gharian 

Vorgath: The Messenger 

Torael: Asmodeus, The Devil 

Kragnir: Caldor 

Rithmia: Gaia 

Aethon : Ados 

Khthon: Xinthris 

Vordu : Janus, The Judge 

Elyria: The Frigid 

Dwarf exoplanet: Zeisis 

 

Once The Consumer wreaked havoc on the Rithmiasphere and two gods were consumed and five were heavily wounded the gods all turned their attention to a single planet. The largest of the rocky planets at the center of the system, Rithmia. Prior to this Gaia had introduced her twelve original beasts yet after all the gods turned their focus to this singular planet mortal life was breathed into Rithmia. 

 

Rithmia from the time before the influence of all the gods is well preserved as the feywild. The shadowfell was created when Gaias soul (divine heart) expelled a planes worth of sorrow.
