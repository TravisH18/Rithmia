# Abaddon
Neutral Evil plane of the daemons and divine domain of Caldor, The Ironlord

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Gehenna, Hades, The Eternal Eclipse 
Alignment | Neutral Evil 
Inhabitants | Daemons, night hags, soul eaters 
Divinities | Caldor 

This plane is a wasteland of infinite and apocalyptic proportions that is home to the daemons. It is dominated by the citadels of the **Four Horseman**, but includes vast stretches of uninhabited wastelands, and smaller holdings of minor warlords and vassals. Abaddon holds the headwaters of the river Styx, which flows from here out through the other lower planes of Hell and The Abyss. Most of Abaddon is unnaturally quiet, like a land depopulated by war or plague, and is covered in a cold, black mist. The air feels heavy and rotten, and what little light exists is provided by a strange, sun-like object in permanent eclipse. Caldor, The Ironlord holds domain over The Cinder Furnace with the horseman of war Szuriel however, he realizes all four horseman are out to destroy all of creation even him, they are not allies.
