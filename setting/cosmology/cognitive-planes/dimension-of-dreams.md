Dimension of Dreams 

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Dreamlands, The Nightmare Realm 
Alignment | Any 
Inhabitants | Leng Ghouls 
Divinities | The Unspeakable One 

Existing somehow within the Ethereal Plane in a semi-permanent realm formed from the slumbering desires or mortals. Some grand nations have been created by Great Old Ones and other Outer Gods simply by dreaming them into reality. These pockets are made of "dreamscapes", demiplanes within the Dreamlands created by a sleeping creature. Typically the dreamer is the only one who can access this dreamscape and therefore the rest of the plane; however, powerful occult magic users have found a way to travel into and out of any dreamscape. The core is a collection of dreamscapes. The only known permanent core dreamscape is one called **Leng**, an unusually stable area that is the divine domain of The Unspeakable, and other beings of the *Elder Mythos*.
