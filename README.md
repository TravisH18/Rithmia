# Rithmia
Complete Repository for the Rithmia Setting. Rithmia started as the background for a animated show I wanted to write when I was in middle school (around 2012). It then morphed into a hombrew D&D 5e campaign setting for an online campaign with my friends during the Covid Pandemic. It became my covid obsession and grew into a setting with over ten thousand years of lore and a place where I want any genre of story to be told. From post-apocalyptic to cyberpunk, to superhero golden age to industrial revolution to something wildly fantastical and everything in between and outside of that. So far it has told stories in these genres.

- Epic Fantasy
- Plague / Zombie apocalypse
- Post-apocalyptic (not related to other apocalypse)
- Steam/bio punk
- Silver age comics
