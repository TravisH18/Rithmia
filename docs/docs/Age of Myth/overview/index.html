Rithmia's Age of Myth

This is the main era that will house a majority of D&D campaigns and it is where the "present" is at the moment Here is where data relavent to the age will be housed. The four degenerates, steampunk, etc. take place here.

Any story any genre​

This period of time spans ~3000 years and has the ability to tell just about any kind of story. A rough timeline below lays out the main theme of each time period as well as inspirations to use for your campaigns. Most importantly a transition catalyst that causes one period to change into the next is outlined. These are often fun pieces to center a campaign around.

Rithmia is high fantasy setting, go nuts but realize the 8 Prime gods are real and are playing nearly 15,000 year game of chess against an eldritch outer god.

Use the Worldwide Factions wherever necessary throughout this age.

Throughout this age the Pillars of Restraint will fall one by one. Starting with the Pillar off the coast of Azula allowing the Kaiju Primagaru to be released near the continent where it sought to destroy the World Tree.

Timeline​

Background​

Dragons came out on top of the war so most of the early empires are led by dragon's. These pockets of grand civilization have unique cultures depending on the dragons who rule them. Some metallic dragons create the first orders of knights/paladins cultivating cultures based on honor, and truths (a place like this is where we find a young Torm who befriends a golden dragon as a young boy). Other's become the first tyrants creating harsh environments where only those puny mortals they favor may survive (think the obsidian dragon who wipes out the red dragons in western Glaushun and enslaves any fire resistant race specifically the fire giants who held the land and dominated some dragons themselves previously. Finally certain dragons bolster a nation of intelligence and forward thinking (like our silver dragon who founds the Master Library in Glaushun after his best friend / lover the mist dragon passes).

Giants civilization is broken and there is no unity amongst the different giant races like there was during the war. The infighting over who is the dominant race becomes a cornerstone of their culture. Some still hold that they need to regroup and tear down these dragon overlords but can never quite bolster enough support amongst all the races.

Genie's almost all retreat into the EEP through newly created elemental vortices that stay behind permanently in the material plane. Elemental power seeps into the land around these portals and Eun'sur, monks, and other scholars gather around to harness their power. Children born near these vortices have a chance of being born with a deep genetic tie to the elements and the first Genasi start being born in Rithmia.

See The elemental war for more details.

0PD - 200PD:​

Dark ages / rebuilding period

Description: Elders of the elemental war die off, leave behind remnants of nations that only knew war for 50 years (that's like 1-2 generation that changes people and brings about the survival mindset of the period). Some areas will be in ruin, some will be better off, but no one will be overly prosperous so rebuilding from scratch is needed.

Think bronze age collapse.

Transition Catalyst: With the defeat of Tiamat and the birth of three new demigods the masses begin to learn to rebuild instead of live in sorrow.

200PD - 700PD​

Medieval age / growth of city-states

Description: First large city states begin to prosper across the various continents. Growth in religions of all kinds inspired by two facts: Three new demigods have been "born", maybe you could be next to be "chosen" to be raised to godhood.

Transition Catalyst: Eun'sur grow in influence in rural areas and begin encouraging the harvesting of Elemental Vortices. Transition of influence from the end of this age and beginning of next from Druid -> Monk -> Wizard. Druid have strong oral traditions, Monks write those traditions down and then Wizards take over monasteries and codify them (or in some cases steal the written works and then teach them to the masses in mage academies).

700PD - 1000PD​

Magical Renaissance / Renaissance / Rise of nations

Aetherpunk / magicpunk themes.

Description: Still medieval inspired but it is a world of magic, airships (powered by Brumestone) and heavy Elemental energy influence In this age the "power" comes from the elements

Magical inventions should be all around think like gallithopters from worlds beyond number, magic cellphones, etc.

City's group together into large nations

Power is displayed via Arcane prowess and magical affinity. Oligarchy's of mages and Mageocracy should be a dominant forms of government throughout Rithmia. As well as powerful theocracies where great paladins, clerics, sorcerers of divine blood rule devout nations.

Sorcerous bloodlines rule in parts of the world. Mage Academies and Bardic colleges become cornerstones of large cities.

Transition Catalyst: Overharvesting of elements and elemental vortices destabilize the planet's magical current (The Weave) causing natural disasters and resource scarcity. Discovery of Etherite leads nations near the iron mountains to begin to electrically power their cities and become powerhouse nations. Mass industrialization begins and arcane ability will no longer be the only way for cities to be "powered" leading to a common man renaissance. The first pillar of restraint is broken immediately before this discovery which is what heightens the motivation for regular people to super charge their civilizations.

1000PD - 1400PD​

Modern / Steampunk

Description: Etherite is spread the world over as a main power source. Etherite's steam powered machines dominate new markets as steam replaces pure elemental energy.

Most faith's begin to dwindle in popularity as cleric/paladin power in terms of guard / warfare is taken up by the common man and machinery.

Factories, clockwork automatons, and brass gadgets dominant the streets.

Pollution, deforestation and other effects of mining for etherite lead to conflict between environmentalists and the new industrialists.

Divide between wealthy industrialists and oppressed workers grows wide.

Transition Catalyst: A great smog caused by new factories causes Eun'sur lead revolt as they charge and destroy massive mines harvesting Etherite. Destroying this mine releases some long hidden underground creature that rampages and destroys a massive nearby metropolis.

In this transition there could be a brief Dieselpunk interlude. This iron age could have many wars sparking technological innovations. This war will be fought with armored tanks and combustion engines but will be won once the power of the sun is unleashed with an Solite and Etherite powered atomic bomb that unleashes massive amounts of lightning, fire and radiance, devastating an massive settlement. This war between industry and nature should be widespread and grueling. Decades of fighting, insiting rebellions and buildup of war-powers bring people to all kinds of religions. Opposers of the Eun'sur follow Caldor, The Ironlord fervently to give them the power to smite their foes for example.

1400PD - 1900 PD​

Solarpunk / Rise in religion

Description: The Church of Gharian scholars discover solar power by using Solite which can be harvested without harmful environmental impacts.

After a secular 600 years the faiths are able to bring people together as mass industrialization has destroyed many communities in the long war of the previous period. Eco-conscious societies embrace renewable energy while some industrialists manipulate and conspire against this new tech to hold on to what wealth they still have. Green communes and decentralized governance replace megacorporation. Some cults worship solar power as a deity of it's own. Masses flock to Jericho's ladder during the Week Day a time in the southern sea where the sun doesn't set for weeks.

This era should be when space travel to the moons of Rithmia is achieved using solar sails space exploration is possible and all the planets of the solar system closer to the sun are visited by Rithmians.

Should be full treasure planet solar punk type period.

Transition Catalyst: "Cult's of darkness" (yes very edgy) conduct a ritual to prolong a solar eclipse. This triggers a global energy crisis, exposing vulnerabilities in this eco-utopia.

Two major responses are:

  1. Create "off-world solar farm" aka a Dyson belt or Dyson swarm surrounding the belt of the sun like Saturn's rings
  2. Make it so energy is not reliant on any outside force and the human body can be the battery of civilization leading to the next era

Towards the end of this era we could have a brief time of atompunk.

1900PD - 2150PD​

Information age

Cyberpunk /y2k futurism

Description: Cybernetic enhancements, data networks, neon-lit megacities. Human-technology merger (can use the x-men merging thing as influence)

A digital underclass revolts against corporate overlords (classic)

Real life tech's like cities being powered by people walking on the sidewalk generating energy should be invented. Also things like people "plugging in" and doing mukbang's but their metabolism is being used to power a building like a generator should be the darker part of this.

Travel between planets becomes regular and a part of everyday life as the brief atompunk period combined with this leads to fusion core powered ships that can traverse the space between planets in just a few hours and travel from Rithmia to the furthest planet in just over a day

Nicola Tesla like global wifi / energy that can be used as a man made Weave (could be a 10th level Weave Enhancement spell).

Arcane Power becomes very prevalent again after this Weave Boost.

Transition Catalyst: A virus corrupts global networks after biotech firms genetically engineer "perfect" humans, sparking revolution of man vs. perfect man. It's revealed that these "perfect" humans are merely cyborgs with A.I. brains and are entirely machine with a flesh suit on top.

2150PD - 2400PD​

Bio punk/ Bio-Digital Age

Description: This is mostly an extension of the previous age of cyberpunk influence and their are many similarities between the two

Things like genetic engineering and living machines (A.I.) made of a metal that can "grow" (___) and repair itself become common place. Scientists study this to create a bio - aether - machine DNA hybrid that can self repair leading to erasure of things like 'dying of old age'

The Weave Boost allows for bio-aether hybrid creations to be possible bigger, stronger, better than golems or constructs of the past.

People integrate tech into their body with prosthetics, implants and other biotechnologies

Mortals make contact with Torm's watchers at the edge of the Solar System and discover the metaphysical barrier created by the prime deities after they discovered the Rithmiasphere nearly 12,500 years ago.

Transition Catalyst: A rogue bioweapon accidentally resurrects ancient Scourge beings thought erased from existence destabilizing the world. The Death of the Demigods event ends with Torm, The Last Demigod being slain. This metaphysical barricade surrounding the Rithmiasphere is broken after Torm's defeat.

2400PD - 2900PD​

Space age / Complete Hybrid punk

Description: Magic-powered cybernetics, solar-fueled biotech, and steampunk inspired artistry in gadgets / tech.

A fragile alliance of mages, engineers, and biohackers govern a patchwork world close to the brink of collapse. Some feel escaping the planet is the only viable option for survival of Rithmians.

Ritmian's make contact through space travel to other planets outside the solar system (portals could be used for world hopping previously). Space Exploration Via Helms is possible. Discovered and utilized by technofeudal companies to create company town in space.

Transistion Catalyst: Ends with a new Cataclysm incited by a group of wizards who conducted a mysterious arcane ritual trapping the world in a permanent statis under a purple moon leading to the Wise men campaign and finally the "release of Yith'Zizctal"

Perhaps an underlying reason is that if the world was not trapped in stasis the full barage of the unspeakable onepower would have invaded the Rithmiasphere.

Key Dates:​

1PD The Dragon Empire is founded helmed by the (currently) oldest living dragon the gold dragon, Ceolruleid, The Unhesitant Protector

48PD The First Phoenix Godking ascends to power, establishes Verubia.

199PD Tiamat's Rise from Avernus and corruption of all adult or younger chromatic dragons.

202PD Tiamat is defeated. Three mortal knights are raised to godhood: Torm, Qandia, and Mawara

203PD Amethyst Enclave is founded.

359PD The future Ghoul Emperor makes a deal with a Black Dragon Matriarch and begins his centuries long takeover of the local magocratic alliance.

439PD The Ghoul Emperor turns the once lush rainforest and swamps of Southwestern Glaushun into the Witherwaste through a vile ritual acending to his position as the undead emperor

684PD Iggwilv releases a demonic horde upon the Stormy Willows

706PD Ulgar conquers the Isle of Werewolves after returning from the Plane of Shadow with the stolen Book of Vile Darkness which he seeks to destroy once and for all.

785PD Four Degenerates campaign begins

790PD Four Degenerates campaign ends with the Scourge Invasion and the defense of the world tree against the Kaiju, Primagaru, who was released when the Cult of the Unspeakable was able to use a sphere of annihiliation to destory a Pillar of Restraint. During the invasion all traces of Scourge on Rithmia are wished away by James, saving the world. Due to the might of this feat alone James is the first mortal in millennia to earn the title Sorcerer Supreme and becomes a permanent member of the Amethyst Enclave.

With the defeat of his herald The Unspeakable One released the remaining 5 of the Seven apocalyptic deities upon the material plane in order to defeat the heralds and mightiest champions of the prime deities.

791PD First meeting with the avatar of wrath the apocalyptic deities are revealed.

807PD First successful brumestone skyship flight across the ocean is completed. (Using electric creatures as a fuel for now).

948PD Etherite discovered in a literal ghost town in the upper jaw of the world where the largest permanent overlap to the ethereal realm exists.

1399PD "Death of the Heralds" event where Heralds of the original 8 deities of Rithmia are slaughtered one by one by the apocalyptic deities, their avatars, and followers.

2399PD "Death of the Demigods" event where the gods raised from mortalilty by the prime gods are hunted down in their own plains and sealed away to stop their intervention in the release of The Consumer.
