Public facing Offshoot - The Theatrum Aeternum​

Founded by Valdemar de Iseni, The Theatrum Aeternum, or “Eternal Theatre,” is an internationally renowned theatrical company known for operating theaters and traveling shows worldwide and even on other planes of existence. The Theatrum has earned its acclaim and influence through the recruitment and training of those who perform to the highest degree of excellence in their trade; Theatre is the “all-inclusive art,” and the Theatrum thrives on this principle - recruiting singers, dancers, actors, writers, artists, strongmen, magicians, acrobats, duelists, and scholars. All these and more help to make the Theatrum’s performances excel beyond the scope and quality of lesser pretenders and reach into spheres of influence ranging from yeomen taverns to royal halls to the courts of devil dukes.

Perceptions of the Theatrum range from casual dismissal, joyous fanaticism, awe, and fear, depending on the character of a local director and their company. But all pay homage to the legacy of Valdemar, whose genius, talent, and toil laid the foundations for the company to expand ever outward in the following decades and centuries.

Only a few individuals know that Valdemar continues to guide and direct the company from the shadows. Valdemar has harnessed the success of his legacy through a unique variation of lich-craft, becoming a playwright lich. By entwining his soul with the plays and legacy of the Theatrum, Valdemar has ensured his unlife so long as the company continues to perform his plays. Valdemar ensures this through contracts with companies licensed with the Theatrum - often to the detriment of new artists trying to gain exposure for new, original work.

Theatrum Aeternum NPCs

Most of the Theatrum’s members are artists of some kind - builders, painters, singers, dancers, actors, writers, and more contribute their talents to each company’s spectacles. The Theatrum also draws the attention of merchants and nobles who may act as patrons for individual artists or share their favor with a company. Certain theaters, however, regularly push the boundaries of spectacle to awe their audiences, employing magicians and warriors to display legendary feats of swordplay, spell, and skilcraft.

Any mage can earn an impressive wage with the Theatrum using anything from simple illusions to gravity-defying spells to enhance the performance of a group of dancers, legendary thieves and rakes often put acrobats with formal training to shame, and in particularly daring shows, swordsmen and warriors may battle a captured or summoned monstrosity for the audience’s enjoyment, harkening to gladiatorial contests. Advertising the inclusion of a famous or infamous adventurer in a show’s cast may also draw crowds of that individual’s supporters and detractors. Since becoming a multi-planar organization, the Theatrum has accrued a wealth of knowledge, attracting scholars and even residents and creatures from other planes to join the Theatrum.

d4 NPC​

  1. A young playwright seeking inspiration for their next play asks the player characters about their adventures and asks to join them on their next outing.
  2. A senior performer who jealously guards against upstarts that threaten to dethrone them as a leading star.
  3. An obsessed fan of one of the company’s performers, who constantly attempts to secure time with the object of their obsession and collect “keepsakes” of their idol.
  4. A director, weary of the company’s recent shows, wants to re-enliven and challenge their audience by introducing a “hint of true danger” by incorporating a captured monster into the next performance.

Theatrum Aeternum Campaign Themes

A Theatrum Aeternum campaign explores themes of intrigue, creative and personal freedom, and what price is too high to create art.   It is quite possible for the Theatrum to be an antagonistic or friendly faction for a party of adventurers from quest to quest, as different regional directors and their companies will adopt unique practices to achieve the faction’s overall goal to spread the influence of the Theatrum. Political allies and adversaries may well be invested in the Theatrum’s success or failure, and rivalries frequently develop between actors, writers, and artists, ranging from friendly competition to deadly sabotage and assassination.

Faction Adventure Hooks

The Theatrum Aeternum Adventure Hooks table suggests a variety of ideas for drawing the party into the glamorous world of the Eternal Theatre.

Faction Adventure Hooks Table

d6 Adventure Hooks​

  1. Patrons of a small town Theatrum subsidiary have lost their valuables during their most recent shows. The theater director hires the party to put a stop to the apparent pickpocketing so that audiences can attend the shows without fear of theft.
  2. When members of a company die from apparent stage accidents, one of the crew members reaches out to the party, fearful that a murderer is stalking the company, or that a curse has befallen them. Can the party find the cause and bring it to an end before the rest of the company falls victim?
  3. An independent company of performers have been rising in fame and fortune among audiences, but they have received threats ordering them to shut down their theater. They reach out to the party to hire them as protection during their grand opening. What no one yet knows is that the Eternal Theatre is behind the threats, and they have more than just hired assassins and saboteurs at their disposal.
  4. The spirit of a deceased actor appears during a show, threatening to throw off the performance. Can the party put an end to the spirit’s impromptu performances? And can they discover what has disturbed their rest?
  5. Mad Molly, director of the Multiversal Muster Subsidiary Company, has received an invitation from Duke Inferi of the lower planes to perform in his court. This show calls for performers who are no strangers to violence, as the devilish audience members frequently participate in the show!
  6. The Theatrum advertises a “millennia defining” event - the performance of a powerful magical ritual in the form of a play. Rumors abound as to what will result from completing the play, and its ritual; Will it grant apotheosis to one of its actors? Open a sealed gateway? Summon a powerful being? Grant a wish? If true, the completion of this play before an audience could change the face of the world, and the party must decide if, and how, they will get involved.
