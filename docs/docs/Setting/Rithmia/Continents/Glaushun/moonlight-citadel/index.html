Moonlight Citadel

Dark fortress with three tall tower spires that are connected with tall stone walls forming a triangular design.

Built by very powerful illusionist wizard (actually Curdelgyth) who seemingly disappeared just before the demonic incursion that took place a century ago. How luck of them.

Place is surrounded by a murky swamp and dark squishy earth that feels like walking on moss at all times.

Originally built by an illusionist

Surrounded by air elementals

If they go to the moonlight citadel they will find a dead elven scout pinned to a tree.

The Mistwalkers​

An assassins guild has taken over this castle as their new base of operations. About the Mist walkers:

Symbol is a dagger that the handle and hand holding it fade to invisible.

Members: 20-30 thieves, assassins, spys

2-3 mages (illusion wizards)

Roll 4d10 to see how many members are there at any given time

Leader Ivan, The Howling Wind is a 5'10 slender air genasi who wears a black button up shirt that is partially undone , two silver necklaces hang down one with a ring of djinn summoning (dead djinn). He wears a dark greenish navy blue duster that has a slight collar to it. Two silver rings connected by a small chain on one hand, and he is covered in ghastly grey tattoos that look like japanese style winds. He carries a long rapier with a dark steel blade that he covers in purple worm poison before battle that lasts one minute on the blades edge (he carries an extra vial on his person).

Assassin with ring of air elemental control (invisible stalkers tho)

Has all the benefits the ring gives.

Villain scheme: Influence win favor of a powerful figure

Curdelgyth noticed the assassins taking up residence in her lair long ago. When poking around her exit hole he found her in human form. Not wanting to blow her cover quickly revealed herself and offered power in turn for helping her. She as disguised herself as a older middle aged (60's) female illusionist mage with bright green eyes and greying hair.
