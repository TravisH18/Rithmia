Father of Spiders

The father of spiders is an odd pale creature. The large pale white body with odd contortions and canals digging in like scars make it look almost like a brain. 8 large limbs extend from the thick bulbous body. Alternating limbs 4 of them a long spindly spider legs with two joints that end in an impossibly sharp spear point. The other 4 limbs look like thick segmented worm body's, reminiscent of tree trunks it picks one of these up pointing it toward a pc you can see it is just a hole of teeth as far deep as you can see. The whole body seems to be bubbling like its boiling, large bubble constantly balloon up on its surface popping and letting eggs fall to the ground.

The father of spiders seems to be bound here magically somehow.

Slaying the father of spiders will cause its corporeal form to fall away leaving just a sphere of annihilation.

Also use it to trigger a Dark star spell in the vicinity DC 18.
