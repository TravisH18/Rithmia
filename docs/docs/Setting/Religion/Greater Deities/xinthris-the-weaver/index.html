Xinthris, The Weaver

Area of ConernResponsibility
** Alignment: **CN
** Domains: **Arcana, Knowledge
** Divine Realm: **The Astral Nexus, Ataxia
** Allies: **Gharian, Ki'una
** Enemies: **Ziesis, Asmodeus, Ados
** Relationships: **Xinthris, The Weaver, is the source of arcane magic and the creator of the ley lines. Xinthris promoted Ki'Una to godhood
** Temples: **Mystical towers, ancient ruins infused with arcane energy, and places where the ley lines intersect are regarded as holy sites for Xinthris.
** Worshippers: **Wizards, sorcerers, oracles, scholars, and seekers of mystical knowledge.
** Sacred Animal: **A light purple and blue spider with Peacock like features (Maratus volans aka the peacock spider)
** Sacred Colors (clergy colors): **Deep Purple and Electric Blue
** Edicts:**Study and practice magic, unravel the mysteries of the universe, respect the balance of fate, share arcane knowledge. Weave new combination of magic
** Anathema: **Tampering with the fabric of reality, disrupting the natural flow of fate. Fearing the unknown.
** Symbols: **A shimmering magical sigil, a spellbook with open pages, often decorated with a collection of stars. A sphere of intertwining lines with an open Eye in the center
** Herald: **The androgynous humanoid herald of The Weaver is made up of green blue and purple mandala patterns all across their skin. A beautiful cloak with an ornate neck piece opens upwards showing a gender neutral face that has a strong jaw and tucked in cheeks and high cheekbones. They have a mix of blue and purple feathers for hair and they don’t have feet they seem to constantly float about two feet off the ground.
** Lore: **Wove the weave around Rithmia allowing "control access" to the arcane energy flowing into the world from other planes. Before the weaver earned her title in an unknown past somewhere in the known universe all magic was primal magic and only druids had access to arcane ability. Then the weaver wove the web of arcane energy that deities, and all inner and outer planes pump magical essence in to allowing arcane and divine magic to be accessible to mortal creatures. One man thought he could do better than this weaver and attempted to usurp the weaver using the power found on a secret scroll. This man had one great rival who was able to stop him before he was able to unravel the weave. This came at the cost of allowing elemental chaos to flood into the prime material plane leading to primordials being freed from containment. The original fool and his name are lost to the ages. However, his rival is known as Lemos, The Enlightened.
** Creations: **Elohim (see pathfinder)
** Mortal Servants: **Stitchers: powerful mages who rework the weave itself in areas where magical disasters have caused dead zones in the weave or other magical mishaps that affect magic due to a break in the weave. These guys often act like magical IT guys who have to travel grand distances for a short stitching ritual or it’s something serious and they must gather a group of stitchers for a grand weave rewrite ritual. Jewel eyes: these are arcane historians who study the present they observe and record what will one day become historical events using divination magic. The most devout craft arcane eyes out of pearls and other gems that constantly record everything they see and save it an special pool of liquid etherite that when another person fully submerges their head in they may observe these recorded events

Scattered Spider: basically a hacker group on the extreme end of chaotic neutral. They use magic to disrupt cities or even the weave itself like arcane hackers in order to sew chaos. Sometimes they do good sometimes they cause harm to average people they don’t care they are just pushing the limits. |
