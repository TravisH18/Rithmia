Sangwena, The Broken

Area of ConernResponsibility
AlignmentLE
DomainsThe Crimson Altar, The Mouth of The River Styx, Nine Hells
Divine Realmreplace
AlliesAsmodeus, Ziesis, Lady of Decay
EnemiesEmos, Gharian
RelationshipsSangwena, The Broken was promoted to godhood by Asmodeus for being the supreme pawn of all mortals. Doing exactly as she's told at all times. Nearing the point of losing her freewill and acting as his vessal in life. The Broken, maintains a complicated alliance with Ziesis due to her affinity for sacrifice. She has an adversarial relationship with Emos due to her utilitarian nature.
TemplesBlood-stained altars, underground crypts, and hidden chambers of ritual serve as sacred sites for Sangwena
WorshippersDark clerics, cultists, those who seek power through sacrifice and manipulation.
Sacred AnimalGoat
EdictsEmbrace the power of blood magic, use sacrifice to achieve one's goals, manipulate others for personal gain. Do not disregard the power any mortal hold in their blood and bones.
AnathemaPreserving life at all costs, valuing selflessness and compassion, avoiding dark rituals.
SymbolsA chalice filled with blood held by scarred hands or some evil worshippers depict a redskinned minotaur with 5 horns sprouting from her head.
Heraldreplace
LoreIn life Sangwena was a half tiefling half minotaur who could draw her linage back to the oldest archdevils and she claims asmodeus himself. Through osteomancy and the powers of the blood domain she extended and gnarlled her horns beyond belief for offensive and defensive purposes. During her mortal life she was the most powerful clerical figure on the planet. Ruling the infernal kingdom of INSERT TIEFLING NATION NAME on the continent of ikathon, as the theocracy entirly devoted to Asmodeus and the plane of hell near the center of the jaws of the world where the heart of Yith'Zizctal's seal is. She gained many followers and with the help of a powerful adventuring party received a shard of pandemonium and as able to reseal Yith'Zizctal in a blood binding vow "As long as blood pumps on this planet and I have my horns atop my head you shall be caged, trapped, imprisoned (Asmodeus' voice echos through)". After feeling unique power deep in her bones as he acted through her Asmodeus recognized her power as his most powerful cleric in nearly ten millenia. Knowing she could challenge any of his fiendish generals he promoted her to godhood instead of forcing her to crawl through the ranks in hell as he did not want to lose his current general to her hands.
