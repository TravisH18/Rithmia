The Scourge

The Scourge are alien creatures from the far realm. They were originally spawned by Yith'Zizctal, The Consumer as a biproduct of its fury and are essentially the blood of the consumer as they were flung from the great old one as the prime deities dealt damage to the creature who attempted to consume the Rithmiasphere.

Now they act independently of The Consumer. Their entire hive mind is focused on releasing their progenitor from its prison.

Scourge Invasion​

The scourge have a 4 step plan to consume all life on a planet turn its surface into star dust and shatter it like glass and spread themselves even further into the galaxy.

The scourge spread through stardust but they ingest the far realms void matter so it is a possibility they emerge from a portal as well

Stage one: the feral stage​

The first scourge broodlings and crawlers emerge from creatures who have ingested stardust (remember kids not even once). The scourge assimilate as many creatures as they can constantly growing and evolving and combining. The true goal of this first stage is to establish a grave mind/elder brain on planet to lead them. They use a variety of creatures as a "host" for this like Dragons, giants, or powerful abberations (aboleths).

Stage two: the coordinated stage​

Scourge evolve far enough to have leaders conducting the hive mind like war generals. Humanoid scourge develop stronger psionic abilities and can mind control mortals making it easier to consume and assimilate subjects. This stage often lasts weeks to months, cults form, rituals are performed where cultists are fed to broodlings and crawlers to create more fully formed scourge thinkers.

Humanoid scourge (thinkers)

Stage three: the burrowing stage​

Scourge dig into the ground and kill all subterranean life and draw life from plants just below the surface turning everything into this grayish purple material that crystallizes into a opaque pink “star dust”

Stage four: the shattering stage​

The burrowed scourge combined into one massive sheet of glass made entirely of stardust. They vibrate all their cells to superheat the planet causing an explosion so great the core of the planet is exposed allowing Yith'zizctal to break free of Gaia's Heart.
