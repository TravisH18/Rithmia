# Demigods of the prime elements
Comprised mostly of dragons that claim domain over "elements" of life as lesser deities. Amongst the pantheon a few outliers preside. Raijin, and Fuijin claim dominion over  wind, lightning and thunder. Stonebone (stone giant) claims dominion over "elemental artistry" and craftsmenship. All other elements are commanded by Dragons. This pantheon is worshipped primarily by druids. Outside of this druids usually only give loyalty to one of the aspects of Eutia 

God of Fire - Ieldrog, The Ember: 

Alignment: Chaotic Evil 

Domains: Fire, Destruction, Chaos 

Divine Realm: The Inferno's Core 

Allies: None specified 

Enemies: None specified 

Relationships: None specified 

Temples: Volcanic calderas, sacred bonfires, and fiery pits serve as places of reverence for Ieldrog. 

Worshippers: Pyromancers, fire mages, blacksmiths, those who embrace the power of destruction. 

Sacred Animal: Salamander 

Sacred Colors (clergy colors): Fiery Red and Charcoal Black 

Edicts: Embrace the power of fire, seek destruction and chaos, harness the flame's fury, unleash devastation. 

Anathema: Suppressing fire, extinguishing flames, fostering peace and stability, valuing preservation over destruction. 

Symbols: A roaring inferno, a dragon's claw wreathed in flames, a burning sun. 

 

God of Earth - Holdrovo, The Rumbler: 

Alignment: Lawful Neutral 

Domains: Earth, Strength, Protection 

Divine Realm: The Granite Citadel 

Allies: Gaia, The Earth (Deity of Nature and Life) 

Enemies: None specified 

Relationships: None specified 

Temples: Rocky caves, mountain peaks, and majestic stone formations serve as sacred grounds for Holdrovo. 

Worshippers: Stonemasons, miners, defenders, those seeking grounding and stability. 

Sacred Animal: Mountain Ram 

Sacred Colors (clergy colors): Earthy Brown and Granite Gray 

Edicts: Embrace the solidity of earth, protect the land and its inhabitants, cultivate inner strength, stand firm against adversity. 

Anathema: Desecrating the earth, causing unnecessary destruction, embracing chaos and instability, weakness in the face of challenges. 

Symbols: A towering mountain, a clenched fist emerging from the ground, a stone fortress. 

 

God of Water - Fyrmolun, The Leviathan: 

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral 

Domains: Water, Destruction, Chaos 

Divine Realm: The Abyssal Depths 

Allies: None specified 

Enemies: None specified 

Relationships: None specified 

Temples: Underwater caves, coral reefs, and hidden grottos serve as sacred sites for Fyrmolun. 

Worshippers: Mariners, sailors, sea mages, those who seek the power of the ocean's fury. 

Sacred Animal: Kraken 

Sacred Colors (clergy colors): Deep Blue and Seafoam Green 

Edicts: Embrace the might of the sea, unleash the fury of the tides, revel in chaos and destruction, wield the power of erosion. 

Anathema: Stagnant water, stillness and calmness, preserving tranquility, avoiding conflict and chaos. 

Symbols: A crashing wave, a coiled tentacle, a seashell filled with swirling water. 

 

God(s) of Air/Lightning - Raijin and Fuijin, The Storm Brothers: 

Alignment: Chaotic Neutral (Raijin), Chaotic Good (Fuijin) 

Domains: Raijin - Lightning, Thunder; Fuijin - Air, Storms, Winds 

Divine Realm: The Celestial Tempest 

Allies: Each other (Raijin and Fuijin) 

Enemies: None specified 

Relationships: Brothers and partners in governing the elements of air and storms 

Temples: High mountaintops, wind-swept plateaus, and sacred sky gardens are revered as holy places for the Storm Brothers. 

Worshippers: Skyship captains, storm chasers, aeromancers, those who seek the exhilaration of storms and the power of the skies. 

Sacred Animal: Thunderbird (Raijin), Swift Falcon (Fuijin) 

Sacred Colors (clergy colors): Sky Blue and Silver (Raijin), Turquoise and Cloud White (Fuijin) 

Edicts: Embrace the wildness of the wind, command the power of storms, ride the lightning, respect the balance of the skies. 

Anathema: Still air, calm weather, suppressing the power of storms, using wind and lightning for destructive purposes. 

Symbols: A lightning bolt piercing the clouds (Raijin), a gust of wind encircling a cloud (Fuijin).
