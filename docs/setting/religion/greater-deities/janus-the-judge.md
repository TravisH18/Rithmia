# Janus, The Judge
| Area of Conern | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Alignment:** | LN |
| **Domains:** | Grave, Fate, Death  |
| **Divine Realm:** | The Veiled Halls, Purgatory  |
| **Allies:** | Ziesis |
| **Enemies:** | Xinthris, Caldor, Gharian |
| **Relationships:** | Brother of Gharian, son of Gaia |
| **Temples** | Graveyards, mausoleums, morgues, and temples of repose serve as sacred grounds where Janus is honored. |
| **Worshippers** | Clerics, morticians, judges, seekers of spiritual guidance, and those who ponder the mysteries of death. Judges and general adjudicators often move in as one of the first temples in small villages in order to keep the peace and uphold local laws. |
| **Sacred Animal** | Black dog |
| **Sacred Colors (clergy colors)** | Steel and Slate grey |
| **Edicts** | Uphold justice, honor the departed, guide souls to their rightful place, accept the inevitability of fate.   |
| **Anathema** | Distorting fate, perverting the natural cycle of life and death, denying justice, causing unnecessary suffering.   |
| **Symbols** | Scales of justice, an hourglass, a key to the afterlife.  |
| **Herald** | The herald of Janus is a large black dog that in bright light is partially transparent and ghostlike. The only non-black features of this creature are the whites of its eyes and teeth. Around its neck is a heavy iron chain with each individual link holding a few keys of various sizes and materials. Black dogs in myth are seen as symbolizing contradictions. They are symbols of guardianship and protection, yet also can be signs of bad omens. Spike like his master is LN. They simply are what should be. Some people who see this creature experience a bad omen because that is their fate. Others feel protected by them because it is not their fate to meet their end. Spike is a bitter enemy of random slaughter, and of undeath. This black dogs favorite chew toys are Lich bones. Spike thinks of Janus as his father, therefore Uncle Gharian is one of his best friends. Gharian, when possible, plays fetch with Spike using bones of the greatest evils to have ever lived.  |
| **Lore** | The god of ends, and destiny. Despises necromancy which in his eyes is fate correctional magic. Rules over the Seven Pools of Purgatory where 'incomplete' souls can essentially marinate in a pool of a pure essence before being sent to their rightful path of reincarnation. Used the mothers divine core to promote Ki'Una to godhood for sparing millions of souls not ready to be judged. |
| **Creations** | Monitors (aeons specifically), Grey knights (based on pathfinder hellknights) |
| **Mortal Servants** | Most followers of the judge work in justice of some kind either as clerics whose professions may include arbiters judges or lawyers. Other work in law enforcement as paladins. Either way these wear baggy grey robes as to not signify any wealth and standing that may alter the judgment of others merely by viewing them once. Some darker members (parts of the grave domain) of The Judges clergy team up with the church of The Reaper and dawn the title Soul Shepherd carrying the symbol of a black sheep dog close to heart. These people exercise their power in sending souls they believe are “due to be reforged” to the afterlife. Some can even call of phantom hounds at will and are some of the few mortals who have met a grim reaper and lived because they are essentially coworkers  |
