# Deities
Deities undergo a process similar to lichdom ascension. However they bind a plane to themselves like a phylactery and don’t feed souls to it instead the domain that they subject to their rule feeds them through mortals simply completing the acts of their edicts. Prayers also feed the deity as well and bolster their strength in a distinct way. This distinction means that a Connection is made between the mortal and the god allowing the god to bring that mortal soul to their plane upon dying ending their cycle of rebirth but again bolstering the control over their domain and plane.
