# Age of Dystopia (2,900PD - Present)
Also known as the time of reckoning
Yith'Zizctal has been imprisoned in the earth for millennia, the apocalyptic deities work to infiltrate the nine hells, and steal the key of Solomon from Asmodeus to unlock the divine seal, sunder the pillars of restraint, and allow The Consumer to rip free from this cage and consume all. 

This is where we find ourselves now. A mysterious baby with 8 divine essences contained within its soul. Four strangers who stumbled upon the arcane nexus where “The Child” was born are now tasked with caring for the baby. 

## Key points of this age 

The oceans begin to dry up and over just the first century of this era deplete by 90%. 

Where ocean was is barren dessert. 

Wherever there are caverns ravines or passages to the underdark should be dank moldy and overgrown with moldy fungus. 

The sun has turned a bright violet color and days are much longer.
