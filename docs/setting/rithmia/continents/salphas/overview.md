# Salphas
This will be a cold land dominated by thick tundra and tall mountains. Giants, fey, and sorrowful undead. Very celtic inspired 

Legends of witches and the occult riddle the area as the witch queen baba yaga invaded https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Baba_Yaga#The_Thrice-Tenth_Kingdom 

This was close to a 3 centuries ago and the baba yaga has seemingly been absent since the founding of her kingdom however according to legend once every 100 years she appears to claim her daughter on the throne as well as all of her new grand babies 

Have the three riders be like wandering bosses that should not be truffled with 

This should have a lot of the enclaves in Bigby's Guide to giants (https://5e.tools/book.html#bgg,4)

## Plot Hooks
Black apple coven: Hags disguised themselves as good aligned fey spirits, dryads, river nymph etc and convince remote farmers they will bless the land and crops to protect them through the winter. 

However the land is cursed rats infest grain storage, acidic rain falls, fruit trees bloom poisonous fruits and apples. This continues until the small villages are desperate and willing to give the fey any offering to redeem the land. After they do the hags simply take the gifts and wait for the people to starve before planting them to create undead blighted evil treants
