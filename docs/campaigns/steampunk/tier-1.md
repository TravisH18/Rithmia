# Arc 1 (Tier 1)
Level 1: Introduction to Verdantmoor - The adventurers are introduced to the valley and its inhabitants, including the local lord who hires them to investigate the mysterious disappearances. They must navigate the valley and gather information on the situation before uncovering the existence of the ancient artifact. 

Level 2: Encounter with Kobolds - As the characters delve deeper into their investigation, they come face-to-face with a group of kobolds who have been drawn to the power of the artifact. They must defeat these creatures and secure any information or clues they may possess. 

Level 3: Discovering the Bandits' Lair - The adventurers track down the bandits responsible for the recent string of robberies in Verdantmoor. They discover that the bandits have also become entranced by the artifact and must defeat their leader to gain control of it. 

Level 4: Infiltration of the Ironclad Scavengers' Base - The characters learn about Captain Zane Ironheart and his rival group of treasure hunters. They infiltrate their base, which is located deep within a hidden cave system, and attempt to steal the artifact before the Ironclad Scavengers can make off with it. 

Level 5: Confronting the Ironclad Scavengers - The climax of this story arc sees the characters racing against time to secure the ancient artifact for themselves as they face off against the rival group of treasure hunters in a thrilling battle. 

# Rival Group
The rival group, known as the Ironclad Scavengers, is led by Captain Zane Ironheart, a battle-hardened mercenary who has made a fortune plundering ancient ruins and lost treasures. His crew consists of:  

1. Kaela Shadowwhisper - A skilled rogue with a penchant for traps and deception. She is an expert at picking locks and infiltrating enemy strongholds ups. 

2. Orion Brightflame - A fiery sorcerer who wields powerful elemental magic, especially when it comes to fire spells. He is often seen sporting a pair of large, brass goggles that give him an air of authority. 

3. Thalion Ironwood - A stoic and disciplined ranger who excels at tracking and ambush tactics. His trusty bow, crafted from the wood of ancient trees, never misses its mark. 

4. Grimgar Smashfist - A burly dwarf barbarian with a fondness for explosives and heavy weapons. He wields a massive iron warhammer that can easily crush bones and shatter walls.
