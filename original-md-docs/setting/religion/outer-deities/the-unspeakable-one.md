| **Lore** | The unspeakable is a singular entity on a higher (5th) dimension. To describe their "body would be like describing a solar system as they have tendrils lashing out from their star-like main body that form planets at the end". When a soul is absorbed and pulled from the body to be absorbed into the unspeakable they experience something similar as when Dante enters the emperium in the divine comedy (white rose of where each petal is an infinite number of blessed souls). A distant malevolent star had found Rithmia and locked in on this new planet of uncorrupted mortals. This star harbors an elder evil – a being as old as the stars themselves. This being is so otherworldly that it is able to read the emotions of the ants (mortals) crawling on this planet. It is unknown whether it has a goal, is all seeing, acting purely on instinct or overly calculated. The elder evil of many names such as the violet flame, the unspeakable, the original sin and the eldest is a star of such ill intent that its mere thoughts and feelings spawn evil deities. The first group is what would later be referred to as The Apocalyptic Deities. At the heart of the unspeakables ideals is indulgence in sin and nihilism. They fully indulge in these sins because they believe their is no point in being any other way. Followers will commit whatever unsightly sin they please because they feel there is no true meaning to be good or to anything at all. Why not have a little fun while painting the world mad. Great old one trapped in the core of the earth is sometimes referred to as “the original sin” but the connection between the two is not clear. The key of stars is just one shard of a massive black crystal obelisk that if reformed and the correct ritual performed can open a gate wide enough for one of the unspeakables planet tendrils to enter the rithmiasphere solar system. In a time before ages, The Unspeakable One awoke. Still groggy this beings light began to spread across all realms of reality. In spite of its efforts the light stopped in a large region of the material plane. The prime deities were holding back the purple light from their new creation, the Rithmiasphere. Agitated by this an idea sprang from The Unspeakable and became his first spawn. The idea was a creature that eradicate anything his light and thus Yith'Zizctal, The Consumer was born. The next idea The Unspeakable One had was a creature that could forcibly "Spread the purple light" and thus the scourge were born. The idea is that once assimilated by a scourge crawler you join the hivemind and are a part of the purple light. |
| **Avatars** | The purple pharaoh this is the purple robed humanoid figure that wears a stark white mask and has galaxies for eyes under the mask. Another avatar is referred to as “the boy” it is a short malnourished human boy probably around 12 years old. Long stringy oily white hair sticks to the boys shoulders, bright violet eyes, a dirty robes and tattered sandals. The boy is covered in blood. This is the first bot sacrificed in his name on Rithmia. |
| **Creations** | Void spawn, The scourge, unclear what his connection is to other far realm creatures like mind flayers and aboleths who seem to bear his colors. |
