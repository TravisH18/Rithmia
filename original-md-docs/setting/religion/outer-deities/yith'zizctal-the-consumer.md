| **Lore** | Yith’Zizctal, the eldest, the aberrant overlord, is said to be The Unspeakable one’s oldest child and the one who brought the scourge to the world to rule it tens of thousands of years ago. It is trapped slumbering in the core of the planet, occasionally sending offspring (tarrasque, other kaiju, Godzilla) to the surface splintering through the ground causing global earthquakes and volcanic eruptions and tsunamis as they breach the surface. This is always preceded by a full year where Yith’Zizctal must “consume” before it can reproduce. It consumes by eating arcane energy forcibly pulling energy from the weave and spellcasters into itself to generate the new life form. This reduces the number of spell slots for a casters highest level. This means it is impossible to cast 9th level spells during this year. This consumption does not affect warlocks whose pact lie with Yith’Zizctal or other outer gods. Some cults have obviously formed in its name. Also some powerful orders believe the planet should try to summon this beast from the core to slay it and stop these offspring that come every millennium. Others believe it would cause a complete wipe of all life rendering it fruitless. Yith’Zizctal has the unique ability to consume a being and wipe all trace of it from existence. Their name may not be said and mention of them is wiped from memory of any mortal being and even books that make reference to them are erased. This is because all mentions of them are transported to the demiplane that occupies the interior of their stomach called Hunger's Void. Part of the two deities from the Rithmiasphere are in Hunger’s Void, specifically two divine sparks reside here meaning if someone may make it here retrieve them and find a way to escape they would escape a new deity. The body’s / husks of these deities are floating in the astral sea and are the closest satellites to the Rithmia sphere. Since all evidence of them has been wiped it is unclear what these deities were but the most likely theory is one was the messenger god, The Diplomat who was the opposite of Asmodeus coin (maybe his brother look up pathfinder lore). The other was the parallel to Gharian and was the god of darkness and cold/winter known The Frigid, this is why dark places of shadow seem to be unregulated by divine intervention and communication between gods and mortals has been unavailable  and why more pagans live on Salphas the coldest continent than any other continent. With The Messenger gone Ados was forced to transport the final two pillars themself. When this being eventually breaks free the gods hope to have the other 7 planets fully equipped with powerful beings ready to slaughter the beast in an instant. This is why they seem eager to pull beings to godhood who can help in the cosmic war being waged. Once The Consumer is slain once and for all they can leave the material plane to their creations as their being ascends once again and the chess becomes 6th dimensional instead of 5th. Yith’Zizctal is a primordial word. It means Alien (Yith) Spiraling Death (Zizctal). Along the seams that connect the pillars of containment are seams where the consumer constantly pushes on the seal that traps it. Occasionally when rituals are done or when the scourge enter a later phase of their floods The Consumer can will a creature out of these seams into the world of Rithmia to wreak havoc. These creatures are often called Kaiju. |
