# Frostrife
The Frostrife, sometimes referred to by outsiders as The White Emptiness is a frozen desert that lies north of the lower jaw mountain range and is racked with harsh frozen winds that batter all sides of this landscape 

Within this desert is a "Kingdom" rules by a coven of powerful witches. These witches aren't all the same kind of hag. Some are fungal hags others night hags and others frost hags (most common)
