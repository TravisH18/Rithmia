# Brhymn
Theocratic state dedicated to Asmodeus. Currently ruled by Sangwena. 

On southern tip of iktothon bordering upper jaw of the world overseeing the pillar of restraint in the southern bile sea. 

Recently has begun a conquest to reclaim the entirety of the Upper Jaw range. 

Separated by two large forests from the rest of the continent this nation is relatively isolationist.
