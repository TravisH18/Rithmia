# Chlorophyte
The Feywild - Chlorophyte 

As with nearly everything that originates from the feywild, chlorophyte is alive. This dark green material originates from densely packed organic materials that lie deep within the ground of the Feywild, infused with the magics that give nearly everything on the plane life, chlorophyte is the simplest of life forms on the plane of existence, hardly considered "living" but able to grow and survive nonetheless. It is basically a metallic plant that does not need soil to "live" 

While underground, this material is sustained by the organic materials within the earth as well as the magic of the plane, however, when removed and given sunlight, chlorophyte glows a much brighter green, and begins to feed. 

Chlorophyte's unique blend of organic and inorganic qualities lend towards its dangerous efficacy as a weapon. Once they have been exposed to any amount of sunlight, chlorophyte weapons will eat away at opponents by implanting chlorophyte spores within them, which spread over time. Chlorophyte armor damages weapons or opponents that make physical contact in the same way, growing exponentially once spores are planted. If not quickly burned away or otherwise disposed of, chlorophyte spores will eventually devour the entire being or object they are planted within, making more chlorophyte in the process.
