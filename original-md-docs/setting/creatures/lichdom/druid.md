# Druid
Potion is normal however Druids are required to split the potion between themselves and pouring some on the ground they have enchanted 

Phylactery is a grove filled with plants animals the Druid has a connection with. At the center of this grove the Druid must enchant the land in the following ways: 

Erect a circle of standing stones. 

Enchant the land with Druid grove for 7 days in a row (at level 7each time). After this one of the Druid grove effect become permenant as a new lair action for the lich 

The ritual is similar to what vampires go through. 

The Druid must bury themselves with in the center of the standing stones they erected through their primal magic and consume half the potion and pour the rest around their new grave. The body dies and the soul is absorbed by the enchanted grove and the body is reborn around 24 hours later. Destroying the grove with fire or blight will stop the lich from reguvanating but will not kill them outright. The lich can spend a full week casting Druid grove again to rebuild their grove before their body fades. If a Druids grove or standing stones are destroyed and not repaired is when Druidic liches descend to demiliches. 

After the ritual the standing stones become immune to physical damage.
