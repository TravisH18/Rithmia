# Pools of Purgatory 
The lawful neutral plane and divine domain of Janus. The last stop on the river of souls

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Trail of Rebirth 
Alignment | Lawful Neutral 
Inhabitants | Psychopomps, Shinigami, Souls, Aeons, Decanines 
Divinities | Janus 

The last stop on the river of souls. Purgatory appears as a vast heptagon with a terrace at each edge that peer into different aspects of the soul, the souls true essence. The landscape is flat, barren, and largely colorless, yet soft songs can be heard radiating from the pools of soul essence.  

Janus, The Judge resides in his domain the Veiled Halls. This grand temple sits in a garden garden of pale yet undying flowers in the center of this plane. The Veiled Hall is a massive gothic cathedral made of ivory. The enourmos front doors are guarded by one of The Judge's most powerful psychopomps simply named "Eight" (Maybe a Fulgati).  Inside the floor is checkered marble white and black tiles. Stark red veils line the interior. Here Janus holds court for each soul brought by the Ferrier Ziesis, The Reaper or one of his psychopomps. Janus decides if each soul should be bound to a certain plane of existence or if it should be reincarnated in the material plane. 

At each edge of the heptagonal plane is a large pool nearly the size of an ocean. These multicolored pools of liquid hold vast quantities of a strange healing material, referred to as Quintessence. A few souls who have lost parts of their soul and need to be repaired stay in purgatory in a pool to heal the fractured soul. 

The pools represent healings needed for a certain character flaw (like the classic 7 sins). Each is overlooked by a high platform where a powerful Aeon called an Akhana. These creatures have four gray arms project from a swirling mass resembling a giant eye, from which a tail-like appendage dangles. These creatures may also roam the multiverse but the Arch-Akhana guard the 7 pools of purgatory. 

Once a soul is rebuilt or when ready for reincarnation they are brought to the veiled hall here is the final pool. Janus and his herald, the black dog and The King of Grim, Spike. Here Janus and the Soul commune with the Pool of Rebirth to decide the best course of action for the soul to grow into one truly strong.   

While psychopomps are the servants of The Reaper, some are created by Janus and stay loyal to The Judge while fulfilling their duty to bring souls of the dead to the afterlife. Chief amongst these is the souls of all dogs are transitioned to Decainies, black dogs with red eyes that play fetch with souls and bones for eternity. Their greatest purpose is to hunt all manner of undead creatures. 

Wandering the grey expanse between the Veiled Halls and the Pools are the Shinigami. Absolute, impartial, and merciless. These creatures are harbingers of death. Their purpose to to impose a quick, deserved death to those who would seek to disrupt eh delicate balance of life. They do not take pleasure in anything. A few Shinigami have been known to go rouge for various reason. Either granting more merciful deaths or more despicable forms of execution and have been swayed to be more lawful good or lawful evil respectfully. Often referred to angels of death by those saved by a Shinigami due to their tendency to be fair arbiters and listen to the voice of the oppressed. They view all mortals as another living being and do not judge (As "that is their masters job"). Shingami's are 9feet tall humanoids with skeletal wings extending from their shoulders and scythes made of bone, often robed in black wearing some large hat or mask that covers their face.  

Various other lawful neutral beings often those associated with domains of death, grave, fate also inhabit the rocky bone like terrain. These include bone/crypt dragons 

Aeons are an inscrutable family of monitors who serve to preserve order and balance throughout the planes. They constantly maintain a balance between pairs of forces beyond the knowledge of mortals.
