# The Nine Hells 
Lawful evil plane and the divine domain of Asmodeus, The Devil 

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Pit, The Nine layers of Hell 
Alignment | Lawful Evil 
Inhabitants | Dvils, Asuras, Hellspawn 
Divinities | Asmodeus 

Inverted cone of nine layers each ruled by an archdevil. It is a plane of sinister wickedness and institutional cruelty, its denizens organized into a strict caste system with a very rigid chain of command. Each of the nine Hells had its own physical laws or properties of matter, but all were inhospitable or deadly to outsider. 

Each layer of the Nine Hells embodies a particular sin, as described in the sections that follow. Feel free to add other sins, but be careful when you do. Some classical interpretations of sins shouldn't be used in any adventure. Others, such as lust or gluttony, should be handled very carefully. Also the depth of the layer does not mean we are claiming that one sin is worse / less evil than another. They were organized by Asmodeus, The Devil so the order may be a plot in and of itself to deceive. 

The order of the levels and sins are as follows: 
1. Avernus, Layer of Anger, The sin of anger fuels wrath, vengeance, and inner turmoil. Ruled by a new Archdevil that Asmodeus has taken a liking to, known simply as The Outcast (one of the apocalyptic deities) 
2. Dis, Layer of Murder, Killing an innocent or family member is evil business that comes home to roost in Dis. Ruled by Archdevil Dispater. 
3. Minauros, Layer of Greed, Ruled by Mammon 
4. Phlegethos, Layer of Jealousy, Ruled by two Archdevils Belial, and Fierna. 
5. Stygia, Layer of Betrayal, Ruled by Levistus 
6. Malbolge, Layer of Oppression, Ruled by Glasya 
7. Maladomini, Layer of Deceit, Ruled by Baalzebul 
8. Cania, Layer of Harm/Cruelty, Ruled by Mephistopheles 
9. Nessus, Layer of Pride, Ruled by Asmodeus, The Devil themselves. Some say this is the original sin which led to all other sins. This is the sins that led to the fall of Asmodeus and thus is his domain. 

## An Unjust Universe 
Some players may be uncomfortable if a soul is consigned to the Nine Hells for a sin when the person so condemned lived a good life in aggregate. However, all the souls consigned to the Nine Hells in this adventure were deceived by Asmodeus. While they committed sins, that wasn't the reason they were consigned to the Nine Hells. However, Asmodeus ensures that the soul is punished as if the sin was the one that defined it.
