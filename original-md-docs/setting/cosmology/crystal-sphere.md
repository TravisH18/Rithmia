# Crystal Sphere 
Surrounding the entirety of the solar system is a metaphysical barrier.  

Torm's Wanderers guard the crystal sphere from malevolent breaches. Evil aligned entities may face a horde of undead with the sole purpose of defending the solar system. 

Torms wanderers are a group of lost souls who in the last moments of their life attempted to redeem themselves for selfish purposes hoping their soul would not be judged to harshly. These liars did not truly rebuke their actions on life they simply attempted to deceive the universe itself and as such are not a part or a semi ethereal border separating the physical from the metaphysical. These wanders bar extraterrestrial entry into the Rithmia sphere.

## Rithmiasphere 

The solar system known as the Rithmiasphere is made up of 4 rocky planets 4 large gassy planets and two dwarf planets. The yellow star in the center is roughly 1.5 time the size of the sun we know our earth orbits. Originally when carving out a space for themselves the gods found a space with  10 planets as there were originally 10 of them. Ziesis and Gharian took it upon themselves to hold domain over the two dwarf planets as they were more interested in greater aspects of the Rithmiasphere as a whole. Gharian focused on harnessing the sun. Ziesis founded the river of souls. A place only they knew about since they were gifted the position of The Reaper by a predecessor who simply told them “Everyone takes a float down the stream eventually.”  

Once The Consumer wrecked havoc on the Rithmiasphere and two gods were consumed and five were heavily wounded the gods all turned their attention to a single planet. The largest of the rocky planets at the center of the system, Rithmia. Prior to this Gaia had introduced her twelve original beasts yet after all the gods turned their focus to this singular planet mortal life was breathed into Rithmia. The deities still have power on all other planets in the Rithmiasphere however these seem remarkably more chaotic than Rithmia and they often find native demigod like powers to worship instead. Another possibility is they worship some distorted version of a prime deity as holy texts do not often leave Rithmia to other planets.
