# Kragnir
Lush overgrown with greenery and rich in minerals this is where a majority of the snakefolk migrated too when the scourge invaded at the end of the reptilian age 

A small, terrestrial world with a breathable atmosphere and vast expanses of crystalline caverns.  

Kragnir's interior is home to glittering quartz deposits, which have been mined by its native inhabitants for centuries. 

Deep underground is a metal that has innate abilities similar to a green lantern ring. Caldor actually created the core of this planet to be sentient and have a will of its own and the metal created to do this can extend its will similar to a lantern ring. When crafted into an item and attuned to the user they gain this ability and basically become a green lantern. 

Once ancient Najara learned about the scourge and in their horror fled Rithmia a vast majority used a planetary gateway to come to Kragnir. Their reptilian nature gave them the ability to thrive in the hot humid rainforest. Here they began to sprawl out and discovered vast deposits of metals until they came into contact with the living metal and have since 2500BD been waging war for control as the living metal has seemingly turned to mindlessly slaughtering any sentient life form in its vicinity. Small subjects of snakefolk have tamed the rock and have started a faction hoping to stop the war and creating a symbiotic relationship between snake and man (could either lead to them returning to Rithmia or could lead to them becoming one with the machine or both) 

For reference this world would be just inside what would be considered the habitable zone for humans so it is very hot average temperatures are around 130-150 degrees
