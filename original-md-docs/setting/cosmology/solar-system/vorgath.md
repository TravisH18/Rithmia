# Vorgath 
Only inhabitants are about 10-12 Titan families literal primordial titan family politics 

A barren, dusty planet with a thin atmosphere. Its surface is pockmarked by numerous impact         

craters, giving it a "blasted" appearance. Vorgath's gravity is relatively weak, making it difficult for inhabitants to establish stable settlements. The weak gravity also forces all natives to be immensly tall.
