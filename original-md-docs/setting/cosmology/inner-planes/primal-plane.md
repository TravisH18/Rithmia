# Primal Plane (aka Fey Realm)
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Plane of Faerie, Pureworld 
Alignment | Any Non-neutral 
Inhabitants | Fey, Eldest, Extreme aspects of mundane life 
Divinities | Archfey courts 

This echo of the material plane is suffused with potent magic, and unrestrained emotions. The Fey realm is a place of unrestrained and awe-inspiring natural beauty. The plane is always bathed in twilight of the setting (or perhaps rising) sun, with lanterns and fireflies providing additional, haunting lights
