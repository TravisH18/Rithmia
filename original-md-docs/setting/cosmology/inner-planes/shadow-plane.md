# Shadowfell / Shadow Plane 
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Plane of Sorrow, Shadowfell 
Alignment | Any Evil alignment 
Inhabitants | Darklings, Shadar-kai, Velstraces, Vampires, Undead 
Divinities | Ziesis 

The Shadow Plane is coexistent with both the Material Plane and Primal Plane, and seems to be a warped reflection of the former. It is a plane where the things born here have their colors, sounds, tastes, and all other forms of perception lessened. 

Ziesis, The Reaper has a resting place here. While they never leave the river of souls they do have a home outside of it. When The Reaper feels it is their time to pass on. They return here. This usually causes great upheaval around the multiverse due to the boat floating souls through the river of souls has no commandeer, so the souls wander lost, and do not always end up where they are supposed to, and they are never on time. 

If the primal plane shows the absolute domination of nature this plane is the absolute domination of industrialization. Complete triumph over nature. Soulless mechanicalization of all things. See movie mad god for inspiration
