# Outsider
An **outsider** is a person or creature not native to the world of Rithmia. It can be used in place of alien for creatures from other planets in the Rithmiasphere. It is most commonly used to denote creatures from another plane of existence. 

The most common distinction between outsiders and other creatures is that outsiders do not possess a mortal *soul*. Upon death their matter is returned to and reconstructed in their plane of origin. Outsiders can only truely be killed when struck down in their plane of origin.
