# Understanding the Divine through Lichdom
Artifact, Book 

Book written by a scholar late in the age of myth. A study to understand what the gods actually were at a base level using the path of lichdom as a study guide of sorts since it was the only way for mortals to achieve immortality without the help of a deity. This scholar studies various classes and how they attain lichdom (mostly spell caster classes like wizard druid sorcerer and warlock) 

Kitalsi the the focus of the Druid section but they also theorize about Ki’Una being a Druid in life based on references to an arch Druid of the circle of the moon whose name has been erased from all texts and is heard as a whisper if spoken in Druidic.
