# Religious Rivalries
It is unknown how these eight specific prime deities first achieved godhood, what order they did it, and how they came to find Rithmiasphere and claim it for their own. But we know they don't all get along

## [Gaia](Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) vs Caldor 
It should be heavily noted in themes and myth that [Gaia](Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) created the planet Rithmia (literally) but once the convergence of the gods arrived on Rithmia Caldor forcibly implanted the seeds of war by putting metals of war below the soil since Caldor is responsible for placing things like iron, adamantium within Rithmia. 

Themes of forcible impregnation and a very masculinity vs femineity type thematic battles. 

## [Janus](Greater%20Deities/janus-the-judge.md) vs [Gharian](Greater%20Deities/gharian-the-jolly.md)
These supposed brother go about things in two wildly different ways.  

## [Torm](Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md) vs [Qandia](Lesser%20Deities/qandia-the-cunning.md) vs [Mawara](Lesser%20Deities/mawara-the-watcher.md) 
Purpose and benefits (if any) of war. 3 differing reasons for war and conquest. 

## [Xinthris](Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) vs [Asmodeus](Greater%20Deities/asmodeus-the-devil.md) 
Of the chaos to order spectrum these two are the furthest two their two respective sides as any two deities. [Asmodeus](Greater%20Deities/asmodeus-the-devil.md) is genuinely sickened that their [cunning](Lesser%20Deities/qandia-the-cunning.md) is nearly identical yet [Xinthris](Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) seems to continually spin webs of randomness. He says the weave could be immutable and have tightly bound laws but [Xinthris](Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) specifically made the web to be malleable.  

## Ados vs [Ziesis](Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) 
While [Ziesis](Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) takes life he has the upmost respect for life but [The Reaper](Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) knows the value of life. Whereas Ados was fundamental to creating the basic physiological blueprint for mortal Rithmians yet now they seemingly are only focused on crafting lifeless machine of law and order for good. They are creating an army of heaven to combat the [Outer Gods](Outer%20Deities/outer-gods.md) who have turned their gaze towards the Rithmiasphere.
