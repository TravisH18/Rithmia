# The Divine Contract
Part of the infernal contract forged during the battle with [the consumer](Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) was that the gods may never truly manifest themselves on [the material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) as that is what [the consumer](Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) truly hungers for. It does not care for eating mortals as that would be like us surviving on granules of seasoning where the gods are a rich meals. Heralds are allowed yet avatars are a grey area some may deem necessary to step in but they may incur wrath from their siblings.
