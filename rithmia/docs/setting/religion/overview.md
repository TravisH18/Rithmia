Only way to truly kill a god is to gain power over a deity in one of its domains. Ex learn the [origins](../Creatures/Dragons/origins.md) of the god of secrets, sneak attack the god of shadows and assassins. This kills the previous god and the challenger takes their place. Another path to godhood is to pass the tests of the star stone(?). 

Deities once walked the planet however they were ignorant to the fact that they were not in fact the apex predators, The [Outer gods](Outer%20Deities/outer-gods.md) seem to be as old as anything else and their spawn, the great old ones only seem able to truly satiate themselves and slumber after consuming a deity. This throws whatever domain that deity rules into absolute chaos.
