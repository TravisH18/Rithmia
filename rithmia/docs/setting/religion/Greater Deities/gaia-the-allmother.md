| **Herald:** | As The Allmother she had two great heralds who originally held dominion over the inner elements as well as all beasts. A great multicolored feathered serpent (like q’uq’umatz kukulkan quezoquatl) named Sylphratl larger than an ancient wyrm held dominion over the sky’s and sea (air and water) and aquatic and avian species. A massive stag (half stag half indrik), ruler of all land animals named Zolnixus. Their hooves could create earth where there was none and their antlers could channel lighting and fire. Both Heralds of Beasts were grand multicolored creatures showing all aspects of the rainbow through their coat or scales yet after the split of The Allmothers psyche both heralds were “blanched” and they have not seen on [the material plane](../../Cosmology/material-plane.md) in millennia. Religious scholars theorize they require much of the injured Gaias energy to summon forth and can only be done a limited number of times. However, before these two heralds came into being Gaia had one daughter from whom all animal life can trace some sort of root back. This creature is named Luca (last universal common ancestor). Nobody knows exactly what it looked like since all of it’s descendants vary so greatly. It was the first animal Gaia molded after purging the ancient yith from the surface of Rithmia |
| **Lore:** | The Allmother was the original deity in charge of the elements but after she was splintered in the fight against Yith’Zizctal her power wavered over time opening the door for a need to grant dominion over the elements to other beings resulting in [the elemental war](../../History/the-elemental-war.md). She is responsible for creating all flora and fauna on Rithmia. Animals were the original mortal creation before the fight with [The Consumer](../Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md). After that the [creation of mortals](../../History/creation-of-mortals.md) transformed and free will was bestowed upon all thereafter as [Xinthris](xinthris-the-weaver.md) and [Gharian](gharian-the-jolly.md) pleaded to Gaia to make them in their own divine image. From this point on mortal beings became an experiment to see who could stand side by side with the deities to protect their creations.  

In her true form The Allmother has a serpentine body with scales like that of tree bark. Her underbelly is pure starlight and positive energy. As her true form slithers flora is permanently left behind as plants and foliage grow from the ground at a rapid pace. Her eyes are pure green orbs with a single white dot in each. She has dull fangs and a flower grows from the end of her tail. She often “sprouts” limbs as needed and they grow from her snake body like massive redwood trees. She is the eldest of all the prime deities. Is said to have been more wrathful in youth, traversing the known universe swallowing entire [crystal sphere](../../Cosmology/rithmiasphere.md) in a single gulp. 

 

Gaia in her all mother form is the eldest of all the deities. [The Reaper](ziesis-the-reaper.md) often claims that he can’t smell death on her. 

 

Gaia was originally meant to hold all of Rithmia as her domain. The original Rithmia before the cataclysm and all of her wonderful blissful creations are now known as fey. This is why fey creatures are both primitive humanoids and spirits of nature because they literally are as that’s what Gaia made them to be when she had control over the elements. 

 

After the battle of containing [The Consumer](../Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) Gaia had to split herself into two aspects, Gaia The Earth, and The Decomposer (sometimes called The Undergrowth). Gaia dwells on the surface The Decomposer in the underdark. 

"Do you believe the one who blooms the flowers and sprouts the trees can't also decay the earth and consume its fruits?" 

If The Earth and The Decomposer ever reform  they will return to their original state “The All-Mother”  |
