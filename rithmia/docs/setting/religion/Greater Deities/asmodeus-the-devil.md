# Asmodeus, The Devil
| Area of Conern | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Alignment** | LE |
| **Domains** | Law, contracts, tyranny, seals, deception |
| **Divine Realm** | Nessus, [The Nine Hells](../../Cosmology/Outer%20Planes/Planes%20of%20Law/the-nine-hells.md)  |
| **Allies** | None |
| **Enemies** | Everyone |
| **Relationships** | Maintains a complex network of temporary alliances, bargains, and deals with various deities, and mortals. Always working towards their own goals. |
| **Temples** | Opulent courts, grand halls of power, and hidden chambers of intrigue are revered as places of worship for Asmodeus.   |
| **Worshippers** | Lawyers, politicians, merchants, those who seek power and advantage, and those willing to make deals with dark forces.   |
| **Sacred Animal** | Serpents |
| **Sacred Colors (clergy colors)** | Fiery Red and Ebony Black   |
| **Edicts** | Uphold contracts and oaths, manipulate the truth to further your own ends, embrace the power of deception and [cunning](../Lesser%20Deities/qandia-the-cunning.md).   |
| **Anathema** | Betraying contracts, exposing hidden truths, acting with honesty and integrity, defying authority.   |
| **Symbols** | Scales of balance, a quill pen dripping with ink, a devil's trident/tuning fork.  |
| **Herald** | The herald of asmodeus is a massive female Pseudechis Porphyriacus (red bellied black snake) that’s upper body often takes the shape of a beautiful Erinyes Devil sometimes nude sometimes in black and red scale mail depending on the purpose of the interaction and who is in their presence.  |
| **Lore** | It’s said that Asmodeus appears to mortals as the most beautiful creature they could imagine. He never knows exactly what that will be like in their eyes but he knows it gives him an advantage. However if you ever meat him on a different plane his true for is a very tall slender man in a suit black suit white shirt red tie. Instead of a pitchfork seen in some iconography he weilds a short cane what ends in a double prong resembling a tuning fork. This fork can be struck to control (paralyze) non deities in his vicinity.  |
| **Creations** | All devils. Devils are the creations of Asmodeus as he was wholly opposed to giving mortals free will. Therefore he created beings who could be bound by contracts and the strict meritocracy of Hell meant his realm would have the best chance of surviving a second round against the invasion of [the scourge](../../Creatures/scourge.md). |
| **Mortal Servants** | Fiendish contractors  (GHMG) and Cursed couriers: Fiendish mail carriers who deliver contracts payments or any other deliverables to the rightful owner. Most often they find the successor of a deceased person who has incurred any devilish debt the recently deceased did not pay out before dying. The letters / contracts they deliver are often marked with blood or scream or both and leave an awful black residue on your hands that is not easy to wash off. |
