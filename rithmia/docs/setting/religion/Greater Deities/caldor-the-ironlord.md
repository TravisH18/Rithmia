# Caldor, The Ironlord
| Area of Conern | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Alignment** | NE |
| **Domains** | War, ambition |
| **Divine Realm** | The Iron Fortress, [Abaddon](../../Cosmology/Outer%20Planes/Planes%20of%20Balance/abaddon.md) |
| **Allies** | [Qandia](../Lesser%20Deities/qandia-the-cunning.md), [Ziesis](ziesis-the-reaper.md) |
| **Enemies** | [Gaia](gaia-the-allmother.md), [Gharian](gharian-the-jolly.md), [Lemos](../Lesser%20Deities/lemos-the-enlightened.md) |
| **Relationships** | Caldor, The Ironlord, is the master of metal and war. They forged the raw ore found in [Gaia](gaia-the-allmother.md)'s crust, shaping it into weapons and tools of conquest. His weapons are not as refined as those Ados, The architect creates. Caldor's creations are much more jagged and crude.   |
| **Temples** | Armories, fortified citadels, and arenas where combat and martial prowess are celebrated serve as Caldor's sacred grounds.   |
| **Worshippers** | Warriors, brutal blacksmiths, conquerors, those who seek dominance and power through martial means.   |
| **Sacred Animal** | Deep grey Rhinoceros   |
| **Sacred Colors (clergy colors)** | Rusty Red and Iron Gray   |
| **Edicts** | Embrace discipline and order, conquer and dominate, forge weapons and armor, seek power through strength. |
| **Anathema** | Promoting peace, nurturing life, showing mercy, using diplomacy instead of force.  |
| **Symbols** | A flaming axe, a towering fortress, an anvil and Warhammer. The axe, anvil, and Warhammer often have chips and shard missing making them look worn out.  |
| **Herald** | For unknown reasons, maybe due to a nefarious deal, the current Herald of Caldor is the Horseman of War; one of the Horseman of Apocalypse from [Abaddon](../../Cosmology/Outer%20Planes/Planes%20of%20Balance/abaddon.md). Their original herald was Therrakul an obsidian rhinoceros who has gone mad for some reason. |
| **Lore** | Is the god who hardened the crust to make the first raw ores. Promoted his chosen the red knight [Qandia](../Lesser%20Deities/qandia-the-cunning.md) to lesser deity status after slaying/banishing Tiamat. |
| **School of Magic** | Caldor is often associated with the school of abjuration since the element that forms the back bone of many abjurative rituals is iron. And there is a decent bit of iron in blood. This means blood binding is a sacred art of his worshippers and the more blood used the casters or otherwise the stronger the bond created. |
