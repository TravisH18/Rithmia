[Gaia](gaia-the-allmother.md) made  first then the suggestion of conciousness and intelligence was suggested by [Xinthris](xinthris-the-weaver.md) in an off handed comment. Gharian taking this literally suggested to [Gaia](gaia-the-allmother.md) "moving forward with mortal creations give them free will". [Gaia](gaia-the-allmother.md) responded "But that will make them too close to us, our brothers see these as tools. Why let them do what they want?" "Think of a creation that can ascend to our level, or higher. Isn’t the possibility of an ally more fun than a bland tool? I urge you to listen to me. Make them in our image." "[Asmodeus](asmodeus-the-devil.md), Caldor, Ados see these as workers, even my brother [Janus](janus-the-judge.md) sees them as incomplete, but they are vital to help us when the malevolent light returns" "Make them in our image Mother". Currently suffering from immense injuries incurred during the containment of The Devourer. Sits on a radiant throne on divine life support. Uses the entirety of his efforts keeping the sun bright and happieness as a possiblity in mortal hearts.  |
