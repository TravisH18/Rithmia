# The Divine Truth
Deities as we know them are beings of higher dimensions who have complete control over a certain aspect of life. Like a type 1 civilization harnesses a star they have bound an idea to their will. If we want to get specific they are each a type 3 individual (roughly 6th dimensional). That begs the question where did they come from. The universe can be viewed as panentheistic since all, even divinity, exist throughout the universe and also outside of it in these outer planes. It is closer to pantheism in truth where the true god the one that created all and allowed others to ascend was the universe itself. This one above all is tireless and continues to expand from its inception as its light spreads. Maybe the purple unspeakable is just the result of the visible light of that end of the color spectrum literally being apart of the divine therefore it may not truly be “evil” it just it. It is natural energy and that is just its nature. It may not even be the only [outer gods](Outer%20Deities/outer-gods.md) maybe each color of the visible light spectrum is a sentient cosmic being that acts as the natural energy of a certain force/emotion. 

Thethenatic silence: when a god dies all mortals forget everything about it and it’s named is wiped from the universe  

Oblivati Mori: Gods are bound by Oblivati Mori to not speak of or evangelize a dead god in any way. Breaking this could cause any number of gods to appear instantly
