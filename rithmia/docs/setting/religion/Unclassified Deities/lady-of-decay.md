# Lady of Decay
Referred to by many names, always associated with fungus, and decomposition.  

Cult known as the Order of Decomposition is dedicated to the natural cycle of death and rebirth and that in the moments closest to death is when you are closest to enlightenment. 

Followers sometimes claim they are those who live in death.  

While overall the followers are neutral there are sadistic sides that kill in order to study decay but they try to be righteous by saying the victims deserved it or it was a mercy kill or some other justification.  

Most of her followers come from or are related to the underdark as this seems to be the only place she can manifest and avatar. Those in the circle of spores are who she seems most favorable towards. Many Druids claim to have been given messages by her in order to prevent unnecessary destruction especially of nature.
