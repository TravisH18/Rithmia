| **Lore** | Crimson knight female red dragon lesser deity of strategy and warfare. Promoted to lesser deity status by the Ironlord. Followers known as the Atlas Vanguard. "The masked guardians protect us from the worlds beyond" The masculine deities of Caldor , Ados, and Gharian don't share many traits except that of being a leader. None exemplifies the essence of a warlord than Qandia. As a mortal known as The Red Blade was a fearless general who led troops of any ancestory and background to conquer vast sweeps of Ikothon and Bredoa. Her hoard known as the marching storm is rumored to have set out moving east from the perilous Hopeless Expanse in west Ikothon and did not turn back until it saw the sea two conquered continents later. Was a Dragonborn fighter in life with a unique red / black stripped pattern that earned her the tiger moniker. Highly regarded in Vaeturnum as that’s where legend claims she hails from   |
