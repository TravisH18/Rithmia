| **Lore** | Crimson knight female red dragon lesser deity of strategy and warfare. Promoted to lesser deity status by the Ironlord. Followers known as the Atlas [Vanguard](../../Rithmia/Worldwide%20Factions/the-vanguard.md). "The masked guardians protect us from the worlds beyond" The masculine deities of Caldor , Ados, and [Gharian](../Greater%20Deities/gharian-the-jolly.md) don't share many traits except that of being a leader. None exemplifies the essence of a warlord than Qandia. As a mortal known as The Red Blade was a fearless general who led troops of any ancestory and background to conquer vast sweeps of [Ikothon](../../Rithmia/Continents/Ikothon/overview.md) and [Bredoa](../../Rithmia/Continents/Bredoa/overview.md). Her hoard known as the marching storm is rumored to have set out moving east from the perilous [Hopeless Expanse](../../Rithmia/Continents/Ikothon/hopeless-expanse.md) in west [Ikothon](../../Rithmia/Continents/Ikothon/overview.md) and did not turn back until it saw the sea two conquered continents later. Was a Dragonborn fighter in life with a unique red / black stripped pattern that earned her the tiger moniker. Highly regarded in [Vaeturnum](../../Rithmia/Continents/Glaushun/vaeturnum.md) as that’s where legend claims she hails from   |
