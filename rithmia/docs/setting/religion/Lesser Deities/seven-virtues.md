
# Seven Divine Virtues
The idea to elevate seven demigods to combat the seven [apocalyptic deities](../Outer%20Deities/apocalyptic-deities.md), who are all a result of the churning of negative energy mixing with the light of The Unspeakable. They were born out of The Unspeakable's pure malevolence and are the pinnacle of power in terms of their respective sins. 

The Rithmian Systems deities have a plan to counteract these gods. Promote heroes who exonerate the exact opposite of these sins. The holy virtues.
