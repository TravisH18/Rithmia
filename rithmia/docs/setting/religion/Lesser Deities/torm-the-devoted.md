| **Lore** | Was a paladin in life. Was not dedicated to any one particular deity but was devoted to upholding justice and protecting the average person. Slayed Tiamat on the back on one of Bahamut's Golden Dragon advisors during first attempt to claim power. Torm is the god of justice and defense . His rise to godhood came when defeating a great extraplanar threat that would have split the world in half. Torm died during this fight with Tiamat but Ados, The Architect promoted him to godhood. Bestowing the domain of justice and duty and enhancing him with the strength to plunge this threat into a cage. That is why Torm is connected with the wanderers that guard the planet's [crystal sphere](../../Cosmology/crystal-sphere.md). That is why the temple of torms coming is building the pact breaker as they are about to start an inquisition looking for "anybody making deals with evil entities" Leader of the “trio of protectors” Acts similar to archangel Michael is the warrior of heaven, leader of army against undead and fiends of the lower planes. Torm's Wanderers guard the [crystal sphere](../../Cosmology/crystal-sphere.md) from malevolent breaches. Evil aligned entities may face a horde of undead with the sole purpose of defending the [solar system](../../Cosmology/Solar%20System/overview.md). Torms wanderers are a group of lost souls who in the last moments of their life attempted to redeem themselves for selfish purposes hoping their soul would not be judged to harshly. These liars did not truly rebuke their actions on life they simply attempted to deceive the universe itself and as such are not a part or a semi ethereal border separating the physical from the metaphysical. These wanders bar extraterrestrial entry into the Rithmia sphere. A secondary goal of these wanderers is to use the power of these lost souls to expand the metaphysical barrier surrounding the Rithmiasphere. This means that the Rithmiasphere and its protection against [The Unspeakable Ones](../Outer%20Deities/the-unspeakable-one.md) will be expanded to new planets and systems. |
