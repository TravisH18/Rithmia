# Shadowfell / Shadow Plane 
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Plane of Sorrow, Shadowfell 
Alignment | Any Evil alignment 
Inhabitants | Darklings, Shadar-kai, Velstraces, Vampires, Undead 
Divinities | [Ziesis](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) 

The Shadow Plane is coexistent with both [the Material Plane](../material-plane.md) and [Primal Plane](primal-plane.md), and seems to be a warped reflection of the former. It is a plane where the things born here have their colors, sounds, tastes, and all other forms of perception lessened. 

[Ziesis, The Reaper](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) has a resting place here. While they never leave the [river of souls](../Transitive%20Planes/river-of-souls.md) they do have a home outside of it. When [The Reaper](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) feels it is their time to pass on. They return here. This usually causes great upheaval around the multiverse due to the boat floating souls through the [river of souls](../Transitive%20Planes/river-of-souls.md) has no commandeer, so the souls wander lost, and do not always end up where they are supposed to, and they are never on time. 

If the [primal plane](primal-plane.md) shows the absolute domination of nature this plane is the absolute domination of industrialization. Complete triumph over nature. Soulless mechanicalization of all things. See movie mad god for inspiration
