# Dimension of Dreams
The dimension of dreams and [dimension of time](dimension-of-time.md) are ethereal cognitive dominions. Just as the outer planes and planetary spheres float in the [astral sea](../Transitive%20Planes/astral-sea.md) these two exists all throughout the [ethereal plane](../Transitive%20Planes/ethereal-plane.md) however are somewhat “close” to the [astral sea](../Transitive%20Planes/astral-sea.md).

Existing somehow within the [Ethereal Plane](../Transitive%20Planes/ethereal-plane.md) in a semi-permanent realm formed from the slumbering desires or mortals. Some grand nations have been created by Great Old Ones and other [Outer Gods](../../Religion/Outer%20Deities/outer-gods.md) simply by dreaming them into reality. These pockets are made of "dreamscapes", demiplanes within the Dreamlands created by a sleeping creature. Typically the dreamer is the only one who can access this dreamscape and therefore the rest of the plane; however, powerful occult magic users have found a way to travel into and out of any dreamscape. The core is a collection of dreamscapes. The only known permanent core dreamscape is one called **Leng**, an unusually stable area that is the divine domain of The Unspeakable, and other beings of the *Elder Mythos*.
