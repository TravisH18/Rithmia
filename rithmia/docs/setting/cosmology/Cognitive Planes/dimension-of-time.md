# Dimension of Time 

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Endless wheel, The Loop 
Alignment | Any
Inhabitants | Hounds of Tindalos, Irii, Siktempora 
Divinities | [The Watcher](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/mawara-the-watcher.md) 

Not much is known about this dimension. Scholars who discover it and are unfortunate enough to find their way to this dimension are nearly always lost to time. Time is soft and relative to every square foot.  

The Dimension of Time cannot be reached by conventional planar travel, and no tuning fork attuned to it is known to exist. Also while travelers don't return their items are known to appear in [the material plane](../material-plane.md) through unknown means. The only known texts about this dimension describe landing on the Desolate Shore, traversing the Viridescent Jungle, crossing the Billowy Sea before their raft 'detaches' from the shore and carries them to a city at the center of The Green Medow called Stethelos. Looming above all buildings is a 225-foot-tall porphyry dome covering the Well of All, source of the anima mundi and the convergence point of the flow of time. Time seems to move slower the closer you get to the Well of All. The longer a planes walker stays in the desolate shore the more time passes outside this plane.
