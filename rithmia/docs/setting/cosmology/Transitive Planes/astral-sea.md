# Astral Sea 
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Cognitive Realm, The Nebula 
Alignment | Any 
Inhabitants | Astral dreadnoughts, astradaemons 
Divinities | [Mawara](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/mawara-the-watcher.md) 

Also known as *The Cognitive Plane* this is the vast cosmic sea that exists in parralel with [the material plane](../material-plane.md). It is a strange and confusing plane where spelljammers roam and connections exist to all outer planes. Weaving through this astral sea is the [River of Souls](river-of-souls.md). A metaphysical river in which souls are ferried by [Ziesis, The Reaper](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) to their reincarnation plane after one of [Ziesis](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md)' servants known as psychopomps has delivered the soul to [Janus, The Judge](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/janus-the-judge.md) in his realm inside Purgatory where all journeys after death begin. It is [Janus](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/janus-the-judge.md)' duty to determine the next phase to the souls reincarnation cycle. This river flows into and out of each plane in the outer sphere and either ends in [the material plane](../material-plane.md) in the mortals next life or in a plane that the soul has strong binds to. While rare it has been known for souls to have been blessed by the elements of the inner sphere. In this case the soul is allowed to flow on the [river of souls](river-of-souls.md) but doesn't make it back to the material and their soul is reborn as a genie in a specific elemental energy plane.
