# Ethereal Plane 
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | Plane of Ghosts, The In-Between 
Alignment | Any Neutral 
Inhabitants | Ghosts, Phase spider, Xill 
Divinities | [Torm](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md) 

Sometimes referred to as *The Spiritual Plane* this ethereal border separates the physical from the metaphysical. This border plane is what separates the material and [primal planes](../Inner%20Planes/primal-plane.md) from the outer sphere. In the Ethereal Plane around the Rithmiasphere is where [Torm](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md)'s Wanderers guard the [solar system](../Solar%20System/overview.md) of extra planar and extraterrestrial threats. Where on the Material where a city might exist, on the Plane of Shadow in its place may be ruins or some twisted representation of that city's populous. Distances do not correlate between the planes, however, so that a person who takes a short walk on the [Shadow Plane](../Inner%20Planes/shadow-plane.md) might find herself transported leagues away after returning to [the Material Plane](../material-plane.md).
