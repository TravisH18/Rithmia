# The Outlands
Neutral outer plane.

Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Interplanar plane, Neutral Lands 
Alignment | Neutral 
Inhabitants | Any Outsider 
Divinities | [Gaia](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) 

The connector of all and first stop on the [river of souls](../../Transitive%20Planes/river-of-souls.md). This infinite plane with all kinds of terrain and creatures in any direction. Has a massive center a spire with the city of Sigil, The City of Doors resting at the top ruled by the lady of pain. Sigil has planar connections to all outer planes and planets of [the material plane](../../material-plane.md). 

Originally divine domain of [the allmother](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) and her twin brother ___ who combined to be the beginning and end of all sentient life before [The Consumer](../../../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) devoured [The Allmothers](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) twin brother and she was split into two. Now no deity holds domain and it a plane of pure neutrality with no intervening force. It is the first stop on the [river of souls](../../Transitive%20Planes/river-of-souls.md). Infinite plane with all kinds of terrain and creatures in any direction. Has a center a spire with the city of sigil resting at the top ruled by the lady of pain, whose origin is largely unknown. The most notable thing about the lady of pain is any who worship her as if she was a deity are banished from sigil and the outlands immediately 

Could take inspiration from marvel / X-men’s otherworld (https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Otherworld) for how this vast plane could be divided up to create a sort of nexus of all the other outer planes. This plane is referred to as The Primeval Place in ancient texts
