# Far Realm
The far realm is what is beyond the stars. Beyond the prime [material plane](material-plane.md). 

Home of the [outer gods](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/outer-gods.md). Beings that transcend space and time. Each based on a light of the visible light spectrum currently the only one to make their presence known to [the material plane](material-plane.md) is the Purple Pharoh, [The Unspeakable One](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/the-unspeakable-one.md) 

If you are wondering if the universe plus an additional 38 planes wasn't enough the *Far Realm* is quite literally everything else. This is a realm of madness, and entropy. Nothing that enters leaves. Most mortals can't fathom what the realm itself manifests as and would not last longer than a few seconds even if no creatures were to find them. 

## Artifacts 
Blade of the Void Walker: A powerful wandering adventurer now nicknamed Void walker Kassadin was the first to open and enter a portal to the far realm. The horrors of the universe corrupted him mind and body to the point where years later he spilled out right where the portal was (possibly as a star spawn of some kind) after being slain his trusted ([residuum](../Godmetals%20and%20Unique%20Elements/residuum.md)) dagger was also corrupted leaving the Blade of the Voidwalker in the space. https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/Blade_of_the_Void_Walker_(5e_Equipment) this was recovered by the city of bourmur using the orrery of the wanderer and at some point ends up in the masters library.
