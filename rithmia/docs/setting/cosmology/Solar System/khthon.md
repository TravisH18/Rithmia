# Khthon
A gas giant of immense power, Khthon's atmosphere crackles with electrical discharges that can 

ignite comets and small asteroids in its vicinity. Its cloudy layers are home to a unique species of aeromancers,       

who harness the planet's energy for their magical arts. These aeromancers, named Alecti (short for Alspidchelone), are gigantic whales who seem to be able to breathe in nearly any environment. Mixing the raw power of ether, air, water, and lightning these are one of the few natural beings with control over electromagnetism and slight control over [pandemonium](../../Godmetals%20and%20Unique%20Elements/pandemonium.md) itself. Created by [Xinthris](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) these sentient creatures have a long lasting oral tradition that spans since their creation. Known for having excellent memories they are kind towards any creature small enough that has never attacked them. They communicate through song which to most sounds like deep rumbling but every vibration length and frequency combination essentially translates to a part of their language.
