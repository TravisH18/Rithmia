# Torael
Hot planet filled with hellish rocky desert landscapes. Oceans of non water (liquid sulfur) 

A rugged, cratered world with a reddish          

hue due to iron oxide deposits in the soil. Its landscape        

is dotted with twisted rock formations and steep canyons. 

This planet is used as a cage for devils so unruly that [asmodeus](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/asmodeus-the-devil.md) banishes them from hell using a specific seal that if they stay here long enough before returning this place becomes their home plane so he can dispose of a not have to worry about keeping them out of his business in hell
