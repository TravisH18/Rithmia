# The Abyss
Chaotic evil plane made up of a near infinite number of layers. There are no gods here. Only demon lords who each rule over their own layer.
Attributes | Description 
--- | --- 
Titles | The Infinite Gorge, Chaos Chasm 
Alignment | Chaotic Evil 
Inhabitants | Demons 
Divinities | Demon Lords 

The chaotic evil plane is dedicated to the rule of evil unfettered by law. The Abyssal realms are pockets in which the fabric of the Abyss has slowed its relentless, changing insanity to a near-reality pace. These places are often ruled by a demon lord, either thanks to the lord's own power over the area, or simply because demon lords are attracted to a place to build a fortress and armies. Sporadically, the demonic occupants of the Abyss will pour from planar rifts to menace one plane or another, and sometimes even tear their way into the Material Plane.
