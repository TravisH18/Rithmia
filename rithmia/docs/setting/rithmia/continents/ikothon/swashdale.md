# Swashdale
This area has eastern African theme with this being the rift valley.  

Possibly a strong isolationist nation like the Ethiopian Empire this being a theocracy of divine soul sorcerers said to be descended from a god (Ados / [Gharian](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gharian-the-jolly.md)). Due to theological differences they are vehemently opposed to the nation of [Brhymn](brhymn.md).
