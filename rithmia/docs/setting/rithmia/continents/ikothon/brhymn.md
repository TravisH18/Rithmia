# Brhymn
Theocratic state dedicated to [Asmodeus](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/asmodeus-the-devil.md). Currently ruled by [Sangwena](../../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/sangwena-the-broken.md). 

On southern tip of iktothon bordering [upper jaw of the world](../Azula/upper-jaw-of-the-world.md) overseeing the pillar of restraint in the southern bile sea. 

Recently has begun a conquest to reclaim the entirety of the Upper Jaw range. 

Separated by two large forests from the rest of the continent this nation is relatively isolationist.
