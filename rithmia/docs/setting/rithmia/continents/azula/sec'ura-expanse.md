# Sec'ura Expanse
Based on the sechura desert in Peru near the coast by the Andes mountains, known as the rainbow mountains. Can also use the atacama desert in chile as inspo for weather 

This should not be a sahara like desert but more like Peru's coastal desert. Rivers from [the Uncanny Steeps](the-uncanny-steeps.md) should run through it. Heavy cold fog should linger in the morning. The rivers should create small oases in an otherwise lifeless land.  

Research the chinchorro people for ideas on society in the area and also Norte Chico (First american urban complex) and Aspero and Caral and Huaricanga
