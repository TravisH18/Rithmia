# Eclipse Reef
Before 790PD this island was known as Dragonisle. Historically it has been the seat of the largest pirate crew on the sea of fallen stars as it is the largest island in the sea.

## Northern Forest
Forest in the island has a treant that is a type of oracle for a small wood elf / dryad village that worships a being named “The Guardian''. The treant is the guardians old friend and acted as a friend/therapist when the guardian called this entire island home. This treant's bark seems to shimmer in the sunlight. Elven archers paired with blink dogs hunt the forest and come across the party fighting something in the forest. The guardian is a guardian naga who created the treant by slumbering atop them for a century. And is currently slumbering. 

Forest Thorp (20-80 people) consists of 

32 Adult Wood elves (15 scouts, 10 archers, 5 druids, 1 diviner wizard, 1 young sorcerer) 

7 blink dogs 

1 treant 

1 guardian naga (slumbering and will wake up if conflict erupts) 

Rumors picked up from the elves. 4 trolls in the forest used to be their biggest competition in the forest. After the elves slayed one they didn’t see the other 2 for months. Recently, 1 massive freak has been roaming the forest.
