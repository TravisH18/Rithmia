# Pool of Echos
Treant Civilization that survives with a trio of oracles, one norn, one leshay, and one baelnorn. 

A pool of crystal clear water that is able of emmulating any divination spell. 

Once home to powerful archey's known as the Trinorn Court since Iggwilv's invasion the pool has been cursed as it was once a nexus to the elemental plane of water. It was twisted and manipulated into a portal to the abyss itself. A curse that allows blight elementals to seep into the land and trees has twisted the area that still occasionally come through the nullified portal. The invasion was led by the witch queen and a powerful balor which left the center of this forest's willow trees with absolutely no greenery that hasn't grown back in 100 years. 

 

Now: a marilith/wastrilith calls the pool home, with a swarm of balguras, hezrous, dretch and even quasits 

Encounter: Curse of the Forest 

 

Background: The cursed forest has become a breeding ground for powerful demons and malevolent spirits, who have been drawn to the dark energy that permeates the area. The adventurers have been tasked with venturing into the cursed forest to locate and destroy the source of the curse. 

Setup: As the adventurers make their way through the twisted landscape of the cursed forest, they begin to encounter increasingly powerful and malevolent creatures. They quickly realize that this is not an ordinary forest and that they are dealing with dark and powerful magic. They must use all of their skills and abilities to survive as they make their way towards the heart of the curse. 

Encounter: The adventurers eventually come to a clearing, where they find a powerful demon, a Marilith, waiting for them. The Marilith has been drawn to the cursed forest by the dark magic, and it has taken up residence in the area. It is surrounded by a group of demonic minions, including quasits, hezrous, and barlguras. 

 

The Marilith is a formidable opponent, wielding multiple swords and able to strike with lightning speed. It is also resistant to most forms of magic, making it difficult for the adventurers to harm it with spells. The minions, while not as powerful as the Marilith, are still deadly and can inflict significant damage if left unchecked. 

In addition to the Marilith and its minions, the cursed forest itself is actively working against the adventurers. The trees and vines seem to come to life, wrapping around the adventurers and slowing their movements. The air is thick with dark magic, making it difficult to see and breathe. 

Conclusion: If the adventurers are successful in defeating the Marilith and its minions, they will have struck a major blow against the source of the curse. However, they will still need to determine the true origin of the curse and put an end to it once and for all. 

Defeating the Marilith will not be an easy task, and it will require all of the adventurers' skill and teamwork. However, if they can emerge victorious, they will be one step closer to lifting the curse and restoring the forest to its former glory.
