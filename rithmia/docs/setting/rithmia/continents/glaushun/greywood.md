# Greywood
West of the [Stormy Willows](stormy-willows.md) across the glooming pass valley that bisects the two forest (grey wood is significantly smaller. 

Used to be home to moon elves who disappeared suddenly. Home to fey creatures, green hag covens, the main portion of the black blood clan (lycanthrope tribes) (sphinx gives lycanthrope trials to allow those to learn to control the curse) 

During the demonic incursion cause by Iggwilv in the Greywood and stormy willows ravaged the Greywood to an unrecognizable state. The moon elves that used to populate the area all disappeared the beautiful brown trees all turned grey.  

Includes blood wood giants as well

## Factions

They kidnap children because the one child they have is a flesh monster. 

Their lair is deep in a twisted labyrinth grove that changes with every turn
