## NPCs 

Avain: Female Air genasi Fighter, Good. Avain has black hair and bright green eyes, and a small mouth. She wears splint mail and wields a spear and heavy crossbow. Avain has an animal companion, a grey wolf named Cathna. 

Marq: Male Elf Druid, Neutral. Marq has auburn hair and brown eyes, and a tribal tattoo on his neck. He wears leather armor and wields a quarterstaff. Marq is absent-minded and serious. 

Florges: Female Elf Mercenary, Evil. Florges is exceptionally beautiful, with red hair and dark blue eyes. She wears studded leather and wields a glaive. Florges speaks with a mousy voice.
