# Tantras 
City on northern Sea of Fallen Starts in Bay of Wardurn

In 785PD city population is ~30,000

## City Militias:
1. City Guard
    1. Regular soldiers who mostly patrol the edges of the city. Guard walls, entrances, manage traffic in and out of city. Watch over bay for invading pirate ships. 
    2. Outfitted with chainmail armor, and hand crossbow, and either a shortsword or a spear.
    3. 10 strong units of crossbowmen guard the harbor and stand watch on the walls where they amn ballista batteries.
    4. City guard base is the *Sea Tower* on the coast.
    5. Total number ~300
2. Trueblades
    1. Sometimes referred to as royal guard. Based in the *Temple of Torms Coming*, upon these are paladins and clerics dedicated to Torm, The Devoted. 
    2. Judicial and main peacekeeping force within the city itself. Perform damage control during warfare, disaster or any such attacks on the city. 
    3. Total number ~50 priests and ~75 paladins
    4. Five ranks in ascending order: Cheval, Marcheon, Whitehawk, Vindicator, and Righteous hand.

#### Torm's Rituals
Torm's followers offered a specific litany of prayers to their god at dawn, noon, dusk, and finally at midnight. They would make additional prayers as warranted, such as in the case of great personal need or crisis. Clerics specifically prayed for their spells at dawn.

Clergy members carried out several different rituals, one on a recurring basis, and the others on special occasions. The ritual of Torm's Table involved offering a bountiful meal to someone in need once every two months, to be enjoyed while the offering priest fasted. Whenever a layperson became a full-fledged priest they completed the ritual of Investiture, and then fulfilled the Holy Vigil whenever they ascended in rank within the church's hierarchy
