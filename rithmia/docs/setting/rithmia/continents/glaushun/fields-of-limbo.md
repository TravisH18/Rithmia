# Fields of Limbo
This entire area has been ravaged by magical energy. Warring nation of mages each led by differing kings of arcane warlords led to utter devastation and a bleak dry landscape where nearly nothing grows. Plague and famine haunts the area but it has the richest [residuum](../../../Godmetals%20and%20Unique%20Elements/residuum.md) deposits on the continent. Seems to be why the Pharoh of ash and dust is attempting to expand into the area. 

In the plains of limbo he hears that the lands were decimated, swords stick up from the land in one valley that was "cleansed/blessed". In this valley of purgatory "Life may enter, but may never leave, death entered once and hasn't left". Some magical energy yet not necessarily arcane energy caused all life to mutated into twisted unknown versions of their past self. When slain these mutans never died "naturally" they disintegrated into a pink opaque dust and this dust would cling to the land and animals walking over it and would begin the cycle of mutation over again. Before the area was cleansed the remnants of the already fractured empires on all sides of the valley sent endless crusades to expel these unnatural creatures created by some magic mutation. This led to humanoids being mutated into undying abberation springing up as well as their swords stayed in the ground as most didn't have opposable fingers anymore. There is a large religious population in the area that are [devoted](../../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md) to [Ziesis, The Reaper](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) who is said to have manifested an avatar over this area of things that never died fully. He came looking for souls when he heard the ringing at death's door, but never found anything. [Ziesis](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md)' avatar outlined the valley of purgatory, sometimes called the land of the spirit swords by the faithful, banished life from entering the area, any living being, no matter its type, that steps foot in the area instantly and permanently becomes a ghost/spirit and is unable to leave the bounded area.
