## [The Vanguard](../../Worldwide%20Factions/the-vanguard.md): Started by Ali, Troph, and Kitalsi.  
This group and its members act as a sort of spy infiltrating the workforce of cities and nations where there is great class disparity. They rally the anger of the workers against those who exploit them. They are the freedom fighters in a necessary violent revolution to end the wealth gap and after the rich are taken down by any means necessary the wealth found is redistributed. Kitalsi has been the one taking charge of the underdark nations. Dismantling the drow nation whose capitol is in Menzoberranzan by rallying the deep gnomes and all slaves who have been raided and captured by the greed of the drow to advance their own familys power within the drow nation. Through Trophs connection to the Monastery of Malevolence and Mercy and his rising idol status, as well as Ali's true nature as a Janni they are easily able to turn the hearts of the lower class. However, just because the ruling class is dismantled doesn't mean the city is a utopia afterwords. Often [The Vanguard](../../Worldwide%20Factions/the-vanguard.md) leaves the local politics to the people who lived there already and this may or may not lead to long lasting prosperity. Sometimes the locals turn to anarchy, other times a true stateless, classless nation is built on the ashes of the old.
