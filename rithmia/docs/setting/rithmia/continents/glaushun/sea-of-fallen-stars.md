# Sea of Fallen Stars
This is where [the elemental war](../../../History/the-elemental-war.md) began. A single mysterious figure arrives to a meeting between the three candidates: Dragons, Giants, Genies, and the gods and challenged them all. They then brought dozens of meteors down destroying mountain ranges and creating the canyon that when filled is the sea of fallen stars. 

## Locations
Kingdom of Lunadora in the west on what was previously known as Dragon Isle. The largest island in the sea was normally held by the strongest (above water) force in the sea. Has been held by dragons, pirate captains, and [four degenerates](../../../../Past%20Campaigns/Four%20Degenerates/Overview.md) notably. 

Vast underwater sea elf kingdom in the east is named Wavecrest. 

In a shadowy isle near the center of the sea is an island held by Ulgar. It has many names, Isle of Werewolves, Shadowisle. Before Ulgar's conquest of the island it had a bridge to a nearby island which was destroyed by Ulgar himself. Ulgar then blanketed the island in a permanent shadow. Ulgar is a somewhat benevolent ancient Vampire lord who spent centuries living in the *Plane of Shadow*. He gained an affinity for those with curses such as vampirism, and lycanthropy. He feels the need to care for people with these afflictions who can not blend in to standard society, like the one he was born into and dearly misses. He did not choose his affliction, it was thrust upon him forcefully. There, in the plane of shadow, he encountered a lich by the name of Vecna. This lich had an artifact named the *Book of Vile Darkness* and was acting like a divine patron, handing out the secrets to lichdom, vampirism, and a host of dark magic to his followers. Ulgar secretly worked under Vecna to get close to and learn about the book, after finding a way to destroy it Ulgar stole it and performed a ritual to transport his entire domain of dread to a part of [the material plane](../../../Cosmology/material-plane.md) Vecna had never been where he hoped to stay in secret for the next century in order to destroy the book, and himself. *For reference, he transported to Rithmia in the year 705PD*.
