# Stormy Willows
Hobgoblins were the largest "civilized" group in the forest besides the elves. The elves had amazing archers, spys, green knights, and mages that were able to keep the hobgoblin attacks at bay. Just before you left you had heard the hobgoblins were retreating to the mountains of the Earthspur range. Now for nearly a thousand years the wood elves have taken residence since isolating themselves in the new growth of the Stormy Willows which sprung up during the war of the elements which created the sea of stars, where there original home was. Displaced they have rebuilt but in doing so have push the goblinoids north towards the mountains. Then a grudge between a green dragon and the witch queen saw the release of hundreds of demons from the once sacred Pool of Echos. Now a large section of the forest is uninhabitable and the goblinoids were further displaced to the mountains.
