# Cragged Savanna 
Harsh hot red desert is the extended blast radius of the [scorched graveyard](scorched-graveyard.md) 

Only country is a mostly Kobold Kingdom. They were once under the control of a ruthless dragon but once they passed the children seeked their own hoard and abandoned the Kobolds who were too weak in their eyes. A few kobold draconic sorcerers and expert tinkerers built tall cities and organized their bretheren into a mighty republic. They constantly vote on issues, big and small, and political power changes hands almost every year.
