# Scorched Graveyard
"Ruled" by a slumbering obsidian greatwyrm, Xaeveintyr (Sovereign of Cinder, The Flaming Desolation, Conqueror of Immolation) who controls a vast amount of fire giants, tieflings, and other fire resistant races with its psionic powers and it sleeps in a pool of lava deep in the largest volcano on the surface of Rithmia. Average temperatures are well over 100 degrees Fahrenheit. Only rivers are those of slow moving magma. 

Fire giants and obsidian crown came from the west, specifically the scorched graveyard where fire giants serve a slumbering obsidian dragon. The king was a puppet and one of these fire giants cut his head off stole the crown and cape in hopes of retreating building the servant of leuk-o and coming back to slay the Obsidian Greatwyrm
