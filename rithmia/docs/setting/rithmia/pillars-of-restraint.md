Forged by [Ados the architect](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ados-the-architect.md) 

Placed into the earth by a previous [reaper](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/ziesis-the-reaper.md) who was a little more masochistic 

[The allmother](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) split herself into two parts and ripped the body of Rithmia open allowing [Gharian](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gharian-the-jolly.md) to smite Yith’Zizctal into a cage crafted by [Caldor the ironlord](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/caldor-the-ironlord.md) 

Sealed with lock and key by [Asmodeus](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/asmodeus-the-devil.md) 

[Xinthris](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) and [Janus](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/janus-the-judge.md) were holding together a tear in reality caused by the death of two deities. 

8 is such a strange number. There were 10 deities originally but two were consumed by Yith’Zizctal consuming their names and all memories of them 

In books detailing the founding of Rithmiaspace there is room for two more gods and when the number of beings who founded this [material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) they say the number is 10. 

Pillars are made of an electromagnetic metal that draws it back to its original form. This is why shards of restraint are drawn back to the pillar they came from. If a pillar were to be destroyed, that would mean the attackers have some way to nullify this property or completely destroy it. 

Around the world 6 ancient pillars of black stone that pierce 500ft in the air serve as the material anchor for the forcecage preventing Yith'Zizctal from escaping the core of the earth. They are hidden but certain black crystal shards that are made of the same strange material as the obelisk seem to call to them like rods of the vonindod. 

Below has been told as myth throughout ages as the Legend of Containment 

The popular belief is that Gaia, The Mother and Xinthris, The Weaver were the two deities responsible for sealing the great old one but they did not do it alone. The key, known as the key of stars is said to be held by Asmodeus, The Devil. 

During the battle between Gaia, Xinthris, and Asmodeus Gaia's mind was shattered into two beings, one still known as Gaia and the other is known as Zuggtmoy. 

These pillars need to be protected as long as all three deities live and do not break their contract the pillars are immune to all kinds of damage except weapons bathed in divine blood (powerful enough celestials would count).  

The pillars creation spelled chaos for immortal beings like the gods. These pillars needed powerful protectors, which is what led to the Elemental war as the planet needed new protectors. 

6 of the oldest gold dragons in existence guard each pillar. This is why gold dragons are not often found in the wild. Some even share the responsibility Of defending the pillars
