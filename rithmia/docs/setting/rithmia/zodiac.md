# Rithmian Zodiac
The zodiac was indirectly created by [Gaia](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md), these were the first creation of theirs on Rithmia and she took inspiration for creation each one from a constelation in the sky.
Rithmia's Zodiac's are based on the following animals: These are often the names of the months amongst druidic circles. 

1. Aquervio - The Koi Waterbearer: Description: The Elemental Waterbearer is an massive fish with a nearly watery form. It carries an amphora filled with liquid starlight, pouring celestial waters to nurture the land. Even though it is called a koi it is the size of a blue whale and acts more akin to the whales we know on Earth. It's body has color patterns that seem to shift with the tides themselves. 

2. Aigleboom- The Thunder Griffon: Fast and fierce. A symbol of freedom. Often associated with lightning storms as their screech pierces like thunder. 

3. Embazio - The Flaming Ram: Description: The Flame Ram is a majestic creature with shimmering, fiery wool that glows like embers. Its powerful horns are adorned with mystical runes that channel the elemental power of fire. 

4. Stonius - The Stone Bull Description: The Stone Bull is a colossal creature made entirely of living stone. Its hooves create seismic tremors as it walks, and its gaze can petrify anything that meets its eyes. 

5. Twilith - The Celestial Dragonfly Description: The Celestial dragonfly is an ethereal beings, each with a body of twinkling stars. They are connected by a physical bioluminescent tether that bonds the two beings which allows them to communicate through celestial whispers. 

6. Luacra - The Copper cobra Description: The Copper cobra is a serpent whose scales are interwoven with electrically charged copper scales. 

7. Soleo - The Solar Lion Description: The Solar Lion is a regal creature with the body of a lion and arcane wings of golden flame. Its eyes emit solar flares, and its roar is said to awaken the sun each morning. 

8. Virdora - The Chanting Raven Description: The Enchanting [Weaver](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) is a humanoid creature with vines for hair, weaving spells into the fabric of reality. She carries a staff adorned with blossoms that bloom with magical energy. 

9. Libraxis - The Ivory Elephant Description: The ivory Elephant is a snow white elephant with brilliant tusks. They embody the element of ice.  

10. Tlincallis - The Shadow Scorpion Description: The Shadow Scorpion is a creature of darkness, its exoskeleton adorned with shadowy patterns. Its sting can induce dreams or nightmares, and it thrives in the depths of the night. 

11. Spirella - The Marsh Mustang Description: The Marsh Mustang is an enourmous horse with beautifully dark brown fur. They embody the element of mud 

12. Crystuav - The Lunar Guardian  Description: The Mountain Guardian is a colossal owl with with black feathers and specs of white dots scattered across their body. It stands as the protector of mountain realms, and its wings are silent and it is overal the most elusive of the group.  It has large illustrious eyes that resonate with ancient magic of the moon Io.
