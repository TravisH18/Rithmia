# Worldwide Factions during the Age of Myth
- Eun'sur, Druidic faction that protect elemental vortecies
- Amethyst Enclave, protectors from far realm and outer planar threats
- The Foundation, nomadic followers of Ados that build infastructure in small villages and towns.
- The Vanguard, revolutionaries that fight the greed of nobility wherever they can
- Winter Owls, adventurers guild that specialize in fighting threats from the shadow plane
