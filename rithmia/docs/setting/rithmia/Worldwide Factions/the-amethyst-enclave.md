# The Amethyst Enclave
Founded shortly after the slaying of Tiamat by the Amethyst Dragon Mount of [Mawara](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/mawara-the-watcher.md) who chose not to follow their rider to their newly crowned demiplane and instead made it a goal to protect and study portals from outer planes in [the material plane](../../Cosmology/material-plane.md).

## Objective: Observe, and study extraplanar portals, and creatures. 

Some are devout followers of [Xinthris](../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) or [Mawara](../../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/mawara-the-watcher.md) and there is a small divide on opinions within the group as the faithful feel it is their duty to eradicate portals where as the curious are more apt to study these tears in reality. They all have agreed to never leave an extraplanar entity alive for study however, they learned that the hard way.
