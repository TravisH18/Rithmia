# Moons of Rithmia 

Rithmia has three moons: Jericho and Aylin (which has rings) are close enough to be dominant over the sky at almost all times of day while the third moon Io shines bright at night. 

## Jericho 

This moon once didn’t orbit Rithmia. It once lied in the center of the planet as it’s core but had to me moved to make room for the great old one Yith’Zizctal, the eldest, the aberrant overlord, is said to be The Unspeakable one’s oldest child and the one who brought aboleths to the world to rule it tens of thousands of years ago. It is trapped slumbering in the core of the planet, occasionally sending offspring (tarrasque, other kaiju, Godzilla) to the surface splintering through the ground causing global earthquakes and volcanic eruptions and tsunamis as they breach the surface. This is always preceded by a full year where Yith’Zizctal must “consume” before it can reproduce. It consumes by eating arcane energy forcibly pulling energy from the weave and spellcasters into itself to generate the new life form. This reduces the number of spell slots for a casters highest level. This means it is impossible to cast 9th level spells during this year. This consumption does not affect warlocks whose pact lie with Yith’Zizctal or other outer gods. Some cults have obviously formed in the name. Also some powerful orders believe the planet should try to summon this beast from the core to slay it and stop these offspring that come every millennium. Others believe it would cause a complete wipe of all life rendering it fruitless. 

## Aylin (ringed) (Dragon hollow moon) 

Rithmias largest moon is always visible in the sky but is illuminated a beautiful pale blue color at night the rings give off shades of iridescent yellow and orange. Basically in geosynchronous orbit. Past this crust is a vast kong vs godzilla like hollow earth full of dragons of all kinds. Pocket dimensions where draconic deities live have permanent portals here, this includes Bahamet and Sardior, the ruby dragon. Major cities: Ruby palace, ( https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ruby_Palace), and Bahamut’s palace ( https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bahamut%27s_Palace) where the seven golden wyrms meet. This lunar body was always hollow and home to the burrowing lunar dragons. Saridor was first to create the ruby palace as an off world domain after the Elemental War. This drew dragons to it as they grew in power and is a kind of oasis to dragons of any gem and metallic variety as well as non-evil chromatic and special dragon. 

## Io  

Io is the furthest moon and is actually a kind of satellite that looks like a regular cratered rocky moon with lunar dragons on the surface. When illuminated at night it glows a soft white almost golden color. This moons lunar cycle is what the calendar is based on. It is also the moon most associated with lycanthropy.
