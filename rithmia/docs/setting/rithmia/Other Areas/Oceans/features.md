# Features of the ocean
Since it really is one big ocean with no distinction beside what parts are near continents these features can go for any part of the waters of rithmia.

## Flying cities
Due to genasi/genie magic there are several floating cities that stay over the ocean for isolation. One grand one should be based on Yjae from the Mythic Realms of Pathfinder PDF

## Jericho's Ladder
Somewhere in the vast southern ocean a mythic pale ivory tower is said to be nearly a thousand feet tall. No one knows where it is as some say it only appears to those “with a clear conscious” or "with a defined purpose" 

This is a spire the was the original nexus to [Gaia](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md)’s home plane. When each divinity was terraforming their planet each one left a specific nexus connecting to their home in the outer planes 

[Gaia](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md)’s original nexus take the user to [The Outlands](../../../Cosmology/Outer%20Planes/Planes%20of%20Balance/outlands.md) maybe even connecting straight to the spire in the center of that plane. Now the gate to [the outlands](../../../Cosmology/Outer%20Planes/Planes%20of%20Balance/outlands.md) is guarded by a sphynx who challenges all those who to games of mind boyd and soul 

This has become a point of pilgrimage for followers of [Gharian](../../../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gharian-the-jolly.md) as an ideal of the faith is “no regrets” so seeing the tower is a proof of clear conscious. It is ultimately an excuse to travel and live as they please.
