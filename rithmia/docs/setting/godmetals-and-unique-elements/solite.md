# Solite
The discovery of this element will be the catalyst for bringing Rithmia into a solar punk age. 

This orange metal has the ability to intake sunlight and channel it into energy. Much like a plant uses sunlight to photosynthesize Solite has an internal chemical (called phosatrape or phoskeraunos) process that converts sunlight into electrical discharge that can be harnessed and routed to power technology only using solar energy and some wires with Solite as the main conduit. 

Initially it will work much like an alternator but throughout the solar age it will be refined to the point where it can be thinned out into a fabric like material and used as sails in flying ships or kept as rigid material and used as the exterior of buildings giving ships and buildings during this age a distinct orange glow.
