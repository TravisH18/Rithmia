# Overview
Due to divine magic interacting with natural elements some were changed or purposefully created with unique features.

Godmetals are natural metals /elements that have specific potent reactions when magic energy is forced into them. 

A more precise name for these metals would be planar metals. They aren't directly created by or come from a deity but they are abundant and naturally occuring in each inner and outer plane.
