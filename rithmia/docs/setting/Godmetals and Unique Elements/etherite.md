# Etherite
This is the element that will propel Rithmia into the industrial age/ steam powered age. 

Etherite is a pale blue metal that when magical energy is applied directly to the metal it super heats itself creating a source of heat more efficient than fire (natural or magical) since the magic energy is converted at nearly 20x the rate to heat. With this artificers are able to create massive steam engines with little magical energy expended and only moderate amounts of Etherite necessary.  

I want to do a whole campaign about discovering this where the party has Godwins level 20 artificer as a group patron roughly 40/50 years after the end of the 4 degens campaign. That axolotl artificer, Shaebyn will have encountered some small bits and realized the magnification powers it hold and the possibilities that can be utilized through its extreme heat conversions capabilities. She will also have made the discovery that she is not the first Rithmian to try to harness this power as she believes the ancient snake empire of Najara used this element in a variety of ways thousands of years ago when that empire spanned the globe following their triumph at the end of the [age of division](../History/age-of-division.md). So she will send the party out on tomb raiding missions to hunt through the ancient ziggurats the dot the landscape of the forgotten coast of [Bredoa](../Rithmia/Continents/Bredoa/overview.md). However, rivals and enemies will pop up to thwart the party on their journey but that won’t be all. The [ancient Najara](../Rithmia/Continents/Glaushun/ruins-of-ancient-najara.md) empire will be revealed to be more advanced than previously thought. Etherite seemed to work as the mundane power source for every day utility but their vast arcane knowledge and a glimpse beyond the Rithmia sphere drove them to widespread panic. And an ancient enemy (who they prophesied to return but has now been wished away) drove them to flee the planet and develop world hopping capabilities. There should be hints that a Najara civilization still exists on a neighboring planet but the translation of Etherite to Aetherite should leave gaps in how this different element allowed for such vast technological leaps over the Etherite power that is just barely being rediscovered.
