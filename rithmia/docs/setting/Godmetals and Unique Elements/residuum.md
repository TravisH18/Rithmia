# Residuum
Residuum is the sprinkle of magic residue left after any spell is cast. Magic users pull aether  from the weave of key lines encircling Rithmia. It is important to note that since the byproduct of magic is residuum that means they are using up the weave. Something’s channel energy back into the weave. Specifically prayers and dedication to [Xinthris](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) but also Druids take time to feed the weave through occult rituals. Planar vortex’s are an odd conduit. They draw energy from the weave and disperse it with a unique energy source connected to the plane. Currently the elemental plane is the strongest of these generators and are most common throughout [the material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md). However if this continues over millennia arcane power won’t be as strong but there will be an excess of this magic element residuum. This is what will eventually lead the humanoids down the path of forging more and more elaborate items out of this material as the weave itself weakens; eventually leading to the ages of steampunk industrialization and even the cyberpunk space age class of weaponry as the world moves into those ages while planar portals close and lessen in number and pure aether becomes a rare commodity 

In terms of [brumestone](brumestone.md) it can work purely off residuum if the residuum is made into a copper wire type material and wrapped around the metal. Like wrapping a crystal in wire and these [brumestone](brumestone.md) clusters can be connected via this residuum wire to create stronger floating machines. 

Residuum is technically a god metal alloy of [Gaia](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md)'s true god metal  

It has a different reaction over time based on the magic it absorbs. It comes from the Elemental energy plane and is most commonly transformed in the EEP but near vortex's to the EEP this metal can seep in the Rithmia's soil. 

the final stage of residuum enhancement is the ability for your items now have the ability to damage and shatter magic items becoming anti-magic of sorts (only works on magic items of rare or lower rating)
