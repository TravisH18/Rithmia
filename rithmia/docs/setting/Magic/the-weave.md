# The Weave
The Rithmiasphere is a very magic setting

## Origins
Each of the original 10 deities is the patron of a school of magic. This is why graviturgy and chronogury are lost forms of magic as there patrons were The messenger and The frigid respectively  

[Xinthris](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) crafted the weave to bestow the gift of magic and spellcasting to the mortals of Rithmia. The weave itself is a blanket of pure arcane energy. It is not made up of one material but all of the gods essences from their various planes and god metals mixed together. When a demigod is raised the weave is partially rewritten to add the new gods essence into the weave itself. 

At its core spell craft is “geometric creativity” all manner of magic is possible by “weaving” together particular symbols/glyphs/runes (somatic components). These weaves are sometimes ingrained in language as a sort of translation of the weave to speech (verbal components). Physical ingredients can sometimes bolster or alter the previous two ingredients to give the final weave enhancement (material components). 

# Backbone Elements
Each school of Magic has a core element that forms the back bones of its more powerful rituals and therefore can lead to enhanced spellcraft if wielding properly by the mage.
