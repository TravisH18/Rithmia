Ooze Armor - 5e 

Armor (any medium or heavy armor), legendary (requires attunement) 

This semi-solid armor, molded from slimes, is semi-transparent. Despite its defined shape, it still has a gelatinous texture that wiggles and jiggles when worn. 

You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. While attuned to this armor, any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes 1d4 acid damage. In addition, any nonmagical weapons made of metal or wood that hit you will corrode. After dealing damage, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative −1 penalty to damage rolls. If its penalty drops to −5, the weapon is destroyed. Nonmagical ammunition made of metal or wood that hits you is destroyed after dealing damage.
