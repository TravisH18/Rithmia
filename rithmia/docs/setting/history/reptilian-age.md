# Reptilian Age (5,000BD - 2,500BD)
Began after the Open Air Crusades where subterranean creatures breached land at the end of [age of division](age-of-division.md) reptilian creatures ruled all aspects of the globe land, sea, underdark, only the dragons ruled the sky. Slowly Najara and xiomorns left [the material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) seeking other planets and planes as their technology advanced and left their mass of creature experiments to roam the underdark. 

Most of the worldwide Najara Empire was either killed by [the scourge](../Creatures/scourge.md) or fled to the outer planes and even different planets in and around the Rithmiasphere. Leaving behind few reptilian ancestors who diverged evolutionarily which is why we have different races now like yuan-ti, snakefolk, lizardfolk to name a few. 

It is important to note that Reptilian humanoids were not the only species alive during this age they were just the vast majority. Of all mortal life on the planet the Reptilians made up roughly 75% of the population. They held an over 85% majority on all continents except [Bredoa](../Rithmia/Continents/Bredoa/overview.md) due to their distaste of its naturally cold climate. 

The Najaran empire became very intelligent. They discovered demiplanes, planar travel method and even interplanetary travel that to this day have not been found again.

Ended due to [scourge](../Creatures/scourge.md) invasion 
This empire seemingly collapsed at the same time the floating cities all crashed and Yith'Zizctal arrived after the scourge were accidentally released in a portal opened to the far realm.
