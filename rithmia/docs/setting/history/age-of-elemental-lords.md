# Age of Elemental Lords (1,200BD - 50BD)
Just before [the elemental war](the-elemental-war.md) 

Odyssey of the dragonlords https://online.anyflip.com/fgoih/xsrz/mobile/index.html#google_vignette 

During this time the dragons who had been hibernating for millenia to grow in strength started to take positions of authority commanding entire countries instead of just fearmongering small folk who lived near their lairs. 

Their was also a “magical build up” in the inner planes as the chaos of the elemental chaos was expanding like a soup about to boil over. Tears from [the material planes](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) opened up around the world. Druids of the land gathered around these nexus’s and due to the excess magic energy pouring the these pools the first genasi were born. These are often deemed first generation (or derogatorily randos) as mortals who got too near the energy for too long were magically transformed. But remember at this time all elemental energies this did not make the first generation born of all the elements but instead one random one. Genasi with more than one elemental lineage were only able to be born after the Taming of the Elemental Chaos.
