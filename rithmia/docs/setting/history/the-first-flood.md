# The First Flood (~8,500PD - 8,400PD)
The First flood refers to a Solar system wide even that followed the legend of containment. During the fight with The Consumer as damage was done to this alien being the chunks of flesh that were cut off were living insectoid cells called the scourge which have the sole purpose of surviving, growing, assimilating strong creatures and reattaching to Yith'Zizctal drawing them to the core of Rithmia. 

During this time the heralds and original (non-mortal) creations of the deities spread across the solar system to wipe out The Scourge, alien beings born from the boiling mass of Yith'Zizctal. 

They were "successful" in the idea that The Scourge did not wipe out all life in the Rithmiasphere. However, it seems on Rithmia due to "The Heartbeat of The Consumer" The Scourge seem to never truly die. They shatter in to small pink crystals and seep into the ground like a malevolent fertilizer. After enough light of The Unspeakable fertilizes these crystals and they are kept alive by the heartbeat of The Consumer the scourge spring forth once more in an attempt to assimilate all life in the Rithmiasphere to be a part of The Unspeakables light.
