# The First Flood (~8,500PD - 8,400PD)
The First flood refers to a [Solar system](../Cosmology/Solar%20System/overview.md) wide even that followed the [legend of containment](legend-of-containment.md). During the fight with [The Consumer](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) as damage was done to this alien being the chunks of flesh that were cut off were living insectoid cells called [the scourge](../Creatures/Outsiders/Far%20Realm/scourge.md) which have the sole purpose of surviving, growing, assimilating strong creatures and reattaching to [Yith'Zizctal](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) drawing them to the core of Rithmia. 

During this time the heralds and original (non-mortal) creations of the deities spread across the [solar system](../Cosmology/Solar%20System/overview.md) to wipe out [The Scourge](../Creatures/Outsiders/Far%20Realm/scourge.md), alien beings born from the boiling mass of [Yith'Zizctal](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md). 

They were "successful" in the idea that [The Scourge](../Creatures/Outsiders/Far%20Realm/scourge.md) did not wipe out all life in the Rithmiasphere. However, it seems on Rithmia due to "The Heartbeat of [The Consumer](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md)" [The Scourge](../Creatures/Outsiders/Far%20Realm/scourge.md) seem to never truly die. They shatter in to small pink crystals and seep into the ground like a malevolent fertilizer. After enough light of The Unspeakable fertilizes these crystals and they are kept alive by the heartbeat of [The Consumer](../Religion/Outer%20Deities/yith%27zizctal-the-consumer.md) [the scourge](../Creatures/Outsiders/Far%20Realm/scourge.md) spring forth once more in an attempt to assimilate all life in the Rithmiasphere to be a part of The Unspeakables light.
