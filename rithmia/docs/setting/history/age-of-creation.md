# Age of Creation (12,000BD - 8,500BD)
This extreamly long age is a time when gods ruled the entire Rithmiasphere. Each deity took charge of one planet inside the [solar system](../Cosmology/Solar%20System/overview.md). See */setting/solar-system* for which governs which. Each deity terraformed, created unconscious creatures, and generally played to their greatest desire. The primordials were birthed as a result of the great divine magic thrust upon the planets. 

## Important events of the age: 

All primordials are “sealed” to create the raw substance of the elemental energy planes. At this point the plane is the "Elemental Chaos"

Some descendants of these primordials were able to hide away through various means and later evolved into the giants we know today. 

Xintris, [The Weaver](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/xinthris-the-weaver.md) begins construction of the weave in order to allow controlled access to arcane abilities that are granted to the people of [the material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) by the extraplanar energies entering through vortexes all around the world. 

Mortals were placed as one group with many variations on the planet of Rithmia as it was the center of the [solar system](../Cosmology/Solar%20System/overview.md). Each ancestry seen by the gods as altered parameters in a grand unsupervised learning problem. They were place on Yadrela, An island that is essentially point nemo the furthest landmass from any main continent. The purpose of mortal creatures was up to interpretation and each deity took the basic physiological blueprint created by Ados, The Architect and began to mold it as they pleased. 

The gods had different ideas of what these mortals should be and how "free" they should be. 

As they could not decide [Gaia, The Allmother](../Religion/Greater%20Deities/gaia-the-allmother.md) birthed new animals and unconscious life like the trees as well. However, the weave was a natural barrier to extraplanar forces they wished to block out from the [far realm](../Cosmology/far-realm.md).  

The [fey realm](../Cosmology/Inner%20Planes/primal-plane.md) is sometimes called the prime world as it is where deities could spawn “rough drafts” of beasts and mortals.
