# Tiamat's Rise (~200PD)
This takes place early in the [Age of Myth](age-of-myth.md)

For centuries to come lesser deities found their way into the daily lives of the citizens of Rithmia. And over the course of a millennia life grew and blossomed into many vast separated civilizations across Rithmia. During this time Tiamat emerged for the first time creating the chromatic dragons and burning parts of the land all across Rithmia. Tiamats fall to the hells marks the first time mortals ever “defeated” (she was banished) a deity. Tiamat left behind other draconic creations like Abishais and various cults. This also marks two important changes in the entirety of Rithmia. Mortals now felt confidence like never before that it was possible for mortals to challenge the gods. Before this no mortal had ever successfully ascended to godhood but after this some tried and maybe some (Like [Torm](../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md)) succeeded. The strength of the gods began to lessen as a wave of people all across the globe grew more secular. 

Tiamat breached into [the material plane](../Cosmology/material-plane.md) and was slain by three great knights who all ascended to be lesser deity’s known as The Protectors happened roughly 80 years ago during the last “[Synchronicity](../Cosmology/synchronicity.md)”. [Torm](../Religion/Lesser%20Deities/torm-the-devoted.md) absorbed the life force of the faithful in Tantras but spared all children in order to create a sphere and protect against the mighty breath of Tiamat. After learning about this in his study of abjuration magic it inspired Tarntassa to build his defense bell in the city.
